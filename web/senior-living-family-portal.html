SANDERS SENIOR LIVING LLC

Family Portal Hub

The family's window into the record — one sign-in, guarded and receipted, through the Sanders platform.

The family portal is where the promise of this house becomes visible: a family does not have to take the care on faith, because the care is a record. Families and clients sign in through the Sanders Dash & Seal platform — the identity gateway of the Sanders Companies — to their own record and nothing else's.

Records, Held the Platform Way

The platform's discipline is Glass-Box: no record, no action. Care-coordination records are carried by reference — the platform holds references and workflow state, never resident health data — and every state change writes its receipt in the same transaction. What the family sees is what the record holds: the care plan, its reviews, and the outcomes recorded against it.
