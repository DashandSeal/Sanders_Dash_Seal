Licensing & Compliance

The company's facts of record, stated plainly — and the rule that what an engagement requires is confirmed in writing before work begins.

This page states S & G Security Inc.'s facts of record, and nothing beyond them. The company is a Wisconsin corporation, founded in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, in 1996; its entity record with the Wisconsin Department of Financial Institutions is S049302. At its founding, the company's registration with the State of Wisconsin, its federal employer identification, and the founder's detective's license and training were all completed within its first weeks — the company has kept its papers in order from its first month, as a matter of founding habit.

Security work is licensed work, and its particulars vary by jurisdiction and by the service an engagement involves. The company's compliance rule is therefore a written one: the licensing and regulatory particulars that apply to a given engagement — the jurisdiction's requirements, the officers' credentials for the post, and the filings the work requires — are confirmed in writing as part of the engagement's confirmation, before the first shift is staffed. A client never has to take this page's word for a particular; they hold it in the engagement's own documents.

The same discipline runs inward. S & G is the corporate headquarters of the Sanders Companies, and the standard it answers to is the house's: documented claims are checked against the recorded transaction before they are answered, and executive actions carry signed receipts under the headquarters integrity office. Compliance, in this house, is not a department. It is the record, kept.
