Uniformed Guard Services

A posted officer, a visible standard, a kept post — the company's founding service, staffed by engagement and confirmed in writing.

A uniformed officer on post is the oldest language security speaks: somebody accountable is here, this place is watched, and the watching is on the record. Uniformed guard service is S & G Security Inc.'s founding line of work — the service the company was named for in 1996 — and it is still delivered the way the company began: deliberately, personally, and under written orders.

Every post is defined before it is staffed. The engagement sets the post's hours, its rounds, its access points, and its escalation path in writing; the officer works to those orders; and the shift is reported into the client's record, not left to memory. Where a post's needs change, the orders change with them — in writing, before the next shift, not after an incident.

Uniformed posts serve the settings named across this website — facilities, healthcare settings, residential communities, construction sites, and events — and each engagement is scoped at consultation and confirmed in writing before the first officer is posted.
