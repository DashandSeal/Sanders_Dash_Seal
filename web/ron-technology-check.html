THE CHECKLIST

Five Requirements

Each item is simple. Together they are the difference between a session and a reschedule.

A Camera

A working camera — phone, tablet, or computer — able to show you and your identification clearly for the length of the session.

A Microphone

Audio the notary can hear without strain. The session is a conversation under oath; it must be audible to be valid.

A Current Browser

An up-to-date browser on a secure (HTTPS) connection. The platform's media surfaces run in the browser — no separate software is installed.

A Connection That Holds

Bandwidth steady enough for live video. If your connection drops calls, it will drop a session — test it first.

Your Identification

Your valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID, in hand — it is analyzed on camera as part of identity verification.
