Residential Community Security

The founding work: a community protected as its own — where S & G began, and the standard it still answers to.

Residential security is where this company started. S & G Security Inc. began in Milwaukee in 1996 as a community business — in the founder's account, one client, then a church, then a business, then people, then communities — founded on a simple ethic: take a stand and protect your own community instead of waiting for somebody else to make it better. That is still the brief for every residential engagement the company takes.

Community security is presence with familiarity: officers who work the same ground, know its entrances and its hours, and are known in return. Coverage is set per community — gates and entrances, patrol patterns, parking and common areas, and the quiet hours — and residents meet the service as a standard kept, not a stranger posted.

The company that staffs a senior living community or a residential neighborhood is the headquarters of a house that includes Sanders Senior Living LLC; it understands that for residents, security is not a service performed nearby — it is part of home. Residential engagements are scoped with the community or its association and confirmed in writing.
