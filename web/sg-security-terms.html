Terms

The terms of record for this website.

This website is the public website of S & G Security Inc., established 1996 — the security company of the Sanders family and the corporate headquarters of the Sanders Companies.

The facts stated on this website are facts of record: the legal names of the four companies, their registrations and establishment dates as stated on their own pages, and their standing under one corporate headquarters. S & G Security Inc. retains the Headquarters seal as its own official seal.

Nothing on this website is itself an engagement. Engagements are entered into with the companies directly; appointment requests made through the Sanders Dash & Seal platform are reviewed and confirmed separately.

The seals shown on this website are the official seals of the Sanders companies, shown as issued. This page states the terms of record in brief; the companies' full legal terms are issued by the companies and their counsel.
