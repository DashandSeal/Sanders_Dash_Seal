About

The record of the company, since 1996.

S & G Security Inc. is the first company of the Sanders house, established 1996 — the security company of the family, and the corporate headquarters of the Sanders Companies.

The house it heads: Sanders Home Healthcare & Caregivers LLC, Sanders Senior Living LLC, and Sanders Legal Helpers LLC, each established 2025, each with its own name, its own seal, and its own colors — and each carrying the same promise this company set first: doing business with us should feel like the best choice you made that day.

By the founder's declaration of November 25, 2026, S & G Security Inc. retains the Headquarters seal as its own official seal — the seal that names all four companies. It is shown on this website as issued, and nowhere altered.

Our family will take great care of your family. The Sanders Way.
