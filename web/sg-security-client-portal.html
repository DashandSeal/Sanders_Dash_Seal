S & G SECURITY INC. · THE SECURITY COMPANY

Client Portal Hub

Your engagement, on the record, under one sign-in: reports, schedules as confirmed, and the incident record — kept the Glass-Box way.

A security engagement produces a record — post orders, shift reports, patrol logs, incident reports, confirmations — and that record belongs to the client as much as to the company. S & G Security Inc. keeps it where the house keeps all of its working records: on the Sanders Dash & Seal platform, behind the client's own sign-in.

The portal is the client's door into that record. Engagements confirmed in writing stand there with their scope and schedules as confirmed; the reports each shift and patrol owe are delivered there as they are made; and when an incident occurs, its record — times, observations, actions — is the same record the client reads, not a summary prepared afterward. Under the house's Glass-Box discipline, there is one record, and both sides of the engagement work from it.

New clients begin before the portal: with a consultation request, through the platform's appointment door or this website's contact page. When an engagement is confirmed, the portal becomes its home. Existing clients sign in below.
