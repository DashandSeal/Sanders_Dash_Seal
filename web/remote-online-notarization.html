THE NATIVE SESSION

Built Here, Not Bolted On

The platform's RON chain is a single fail-closed sequence. If any link cannot be completed, the act does not proceed.

Identity, Verified First

Before any session, the signer's identity is put through credential analysis and proofing, and the decision is recorded. No verified identity, no session.

A Recorded Session

The notarization takes place in the platform's own session room, with consent recorded and the audio-video record indexed to the case.

A Journaled Act

The completed act enters the platform's evidence and journal record — the same Glass-Box discipline as every other act the house performs.

AVAILABILITY, STATED PLAINLY

Jurisdiction Governs

Whether a document may be notarized remotely depends on the jurisdiction and on current authorization. That is the platform's own rule, and it is not waived by a booking form.

Requests Are Reviewed

Choosing RON at booking opens a case for human review. The review confirms the service, the jurisdiction, and the method before anything is scheduled.

Nothing Is Promised Early

A request is not a confirmed session. Confirmation — with the itemized quote — comes from the review, in writing, through your portal timeline.

The Alternative Stands

If remote notarization is not authorized for your jurisdiction, the reviewer confirms the mobile path instead. The customer's election governs; the record shows which path was taken.

NATIVE BY DESIGN

The session system is the platform's own — Sanders Dash & Seal performs its remote work on architecture it designed, under its own authority records and its own journal.
