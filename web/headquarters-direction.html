S & G SECURITY INC. · CORPORATE HEADQUARTERS

The Direction of the Sanders Family of Companies

This is the direction the Sanders Family of Companies is going — stated by the founder himself, plainly, in his own words.

AN HONEST ANSWER

Asked where this family is going — where the corporations are going — Roosevelt Sanders does not invent a destination. His answer, in his own words: “That’s an honest question, and I honestly don’t know — but I’m going to enjoy the ride getting there. I’m going to enjoy every struggle, every victory.”

The building has not been easy, and this page does not pretend otherwise. There were sleepless nights, cold nights, nights at the library, nights falling asleep in a car — something in him, he says, just wouldn’t let him rest — and then waking up and starting all over again, getting the businesses and everything in line. The direction of this house was set the same way the house was built: by not stopping.

WHAT HE CAN SEE

Seen Clearly, Even From Here

United as One

The four corporations united as one — one family of companies, one standard, one family name on every door.

Thirty, Forty Years

A family grown. He can see the success and the struggles both — even the hard seasons it will go through — carried the way a family carries them: together.

Never Give Up on Each Other

“We’ll never give up on each other — much like a family that cares.” That is the long-range plan of this house, stated whole.

It Unfolded

Nothing here was forced, pushed, or dragged. It unfolded — and when things unfold, the founder believes, it means it was meant to happen.

THE METHOD

What He Did Was Care

How did a security company in Milwaukee become a family of companies and an ecosystem of platforms? The founder’s account names no strategy but this: “I didn’t do anything other than care. I didn’t do anything other than show dignity. It wasn’t anything other than I showed simple respect. I treated a person. I didn’t treat the condition. The condition is not the person. And that’s what these companies are about.”

Everything in this house is downstream of that sentence. The companies exist because a person deserved to be heard; the platforms exist so the industries around a person can talk to each other; the standard exists so that how a person is treated does not depend on which door they came through.

BUILT FOR THE JOURNEY

The Design the Direction Runs On

140 Platforms, Connected

140 distinct RTSA platforms, designed to support interconnected human and commercial workflows — the industries around a person, talking to each other in real time.

Subscriptions

A subscription business model, designed to convert operational capabilities into recurring revenue — premium service, at what the market can bear, with no free tiers.

Global By Design

Global By Design is an architectural foundation, not a future expansion strategy — native by design, across seven continents and 195 countries and territories.

One Standard

The Sanders Royal Enterprise Standard — the corporate identity and customer experience connecting everything, on every page and every platform.

THIS COMPANY

Headquarters Holds the Direction

Headquarters’ place in that direction is to hold the house together — one standard, one record, one family name — so that wherever the ride goes, it goes there united. The direction statement of the whole family stands on this website because this is the house’s seat: what is said here is said for all four companies.

The objective was never to build websites for the sake of having websites. It was — and it remains — to make a global operating ecosystem accessible to humanity, and commercially sustainable. Where these companies are going, the founder has said honestly, he does not know. He will enjoy the ride. He will enjoy the journey. And he will enjoy the growth.
