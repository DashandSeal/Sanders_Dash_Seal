Service Area

Where engagements are taken — and how an area is confirmed before the company commits an officer to it.

S & G Security Inc. was founded in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, and its first ground was its own neighborhood: the community around the founder's home, a short street from the district police station. The company's roots stay in Milwaukee; its house has grown — the companies under this headquarters are seated in Colorado, and the founder's office sits with them — and the company's engagements are taken where they can be served to standard.

That last phrase is the operative one, and it is deliberate. A security company that promises an area it cannot staff to standard is selling a map, not a service. S & G therefore confirms its service area the way it confirms everything: per engagement, in writing. The consultation establishes the ground; the written confirmation names it; and an engagement is only accepted where the company can put a screened, trained, supervised officer on the ground it has promised.

The architecture the company stands on is Global By Design — the Sanders Companies are built for the world, on all of it — and the company's growth follows that design as engagements and officers allow. But design is not a coverage claim: what covers your ground is the confirmation with your name on it, and that is the only kind this company issues.
