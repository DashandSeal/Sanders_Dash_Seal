About the Headquarters

The Sanders Companies' story and structure — a family house founded in 1996, and built to be inherited.

The Sanders Companies begin with S & G Security Inc., established in 1996. For three decades the house stood on one company's name; in 2025 it took the shape it holds today, when Sanders Home Healthcare & Caregivers LLC, Sanders Senior Living LLC, and Sanders Legal Helpers LLC were established under the same headquarters — four companies, one family, each with its own name, its own seal, and its own colors.

One Platform, Built by Hand

In November 2025 the founder began the house's platform architecture with a single RTSA platform and its six original industry codes. The work that followed — recovery, design, and construction by the founder's own hand — became the Sovereign RTSA Ecosystem: 140 distinct platforms, designed to support interconnected human and commercial workflows across the house's companies and beyond them.

The structure is deliberate. Headquarters sets the standard and keeps the register; the companies serve their customers under their own doors; the flagship platform, Sanders Dash & Seal, carries the house's scheduling, portal, and payment journeys. Nothing in the house is a franchise, a white label, or a resold product — the architecture is native, designed and built for this family of companies.
