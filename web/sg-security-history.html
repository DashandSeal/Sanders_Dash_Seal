The History of S & G Security Inc.

The oldest story in the house: a father of three, a woman who said sure, a home headquarters in Milwaukee — and a company that protected its own community.

1996 — Sanders and Greer

S & G Security Inc. was founded in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, in 1996. The S is Sanders — Roosevelt Sanders. The G is Greer — Willie Greer, the founder’s uncle’s girlfriend, an amazing woman who helped him in many ways. When he decided to start the company, he asked her whether she would like to start it with him: the two of them did security work for other folks — they could do the same thing for themselves. She looked at him — he thinks she thought he was playing — and she said sure. From the moment she said it, he was on the phone, the computer, and everywhere else with the State of Wisconsin: the detective’s license, the agency’s registration, all of it. About two to three weeks later — he is not certain of the exact details anymore — he had the EIN, the company registered with the State of Wisconsin, the license, and the training. Willie Greer has since passed; her initial stays in the company’s name, permanently.

The founder himself was a father of three, working security jobs and going to college, finishing one of his marketing degrees. He started the company because he was tired of working for other people — and because his children needed something to believe in about him. The decision, in his account, came the night he fell asleep at a job site: that was enough to know it was time to move from being an employee to being a boss. S & G opened for business from his home — that was the headquarters — near Milwaukee’s District 7 police station.

A Community Business

The work grew the way the founder tells it: it started out with one client — whose name he does not give — then it became a church, then it became a business, then it became people, and it became communities. What stood out most to him was that it was a community business: he was part of the community, and security tied right into it, in an area with real gang issues, where security was genuinely needed. The police were there, and they did an amazing job — being an officer of the law, on any level, is hard — but they did not have enough people. He believed in protecting his community, and those within it, himself: “You need to be able to take a stand, or believe in something, other than somebody else will do it, somebody else will take care of it, somebody else will make it better — because if you’re always thinking like that, then nothing ever gets better.”

Strong Until Around 2009

S & G went strong for Milwaukee from 1996 until around 2009. Then the company went quiet, and the founder’s life moved: a thirty-year marriage ended in divorce in 2012 — May of 2012, as he recalls it — and, his children grown, he relocated to Colorado Springs, Colorado. The company waited. Companies founded on a stand do not have to shout to still be standing.

The Headquarters of the Family

In 2025 the family formed under it: Sanders Home Healthcare & Caregivers LLC, Sanders Senior Living LLC, and Sanders Legal Helpers LLC, born within about three weeks of each other out of one man’s need to be heard — and S & G Security Inc., the first company, became what it is today: the corporate headquarters of the Sanders Companies. By the founder’s declaration, it retains the Headquarters seal as its own official seal. Its registry fact of record: a Wisconsin corporation, Wisconsin Department of Financial Institutions entity S049302. The community ethic it started with — take a stand, protect your own — is now the ethic of the whole house.
