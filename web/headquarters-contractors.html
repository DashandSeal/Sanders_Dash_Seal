THE MODEL

How the House Engages

Every company of the house works through independent contractors: caregivers with Sanders Home Healthcare, officers with S & G Security Inc., specialists across the family. Engagements are confirmed in writing; the standards are the company's own, published on its site; and the record of the work — reports, acknowledgments, selections — is kept the Glass-Box way, where the contractor can stand in front of it.

New to the house? Place an expression of interest below. It enters the same review queue as every portal record: a person reads it, and the answer comes from a person. An expression of interest is not an offer, and no engagement exists until it is confirmed in writing.
