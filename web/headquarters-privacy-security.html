Privacy & Security Program

Sovereignty, Glass-Box, native by design — the doctrine that governs how the house holds information.

The platforms of the Sanders Companies are built reference-only: they hold references and workflow state — the fact that something happened, when, and under whose authority — not the underlying personal records themselves. Where a platform touches care, it is HIPAA-by-design: the Home Healthcare platform keeps no fields for names, diagnoses, or free-text health information, and the Senior Living platform holds no resident health data. The discipline has a name in the house: Glass-Box — no record, no action; no record, no access.

Fail-Closed, and Native

The house's systems are fail-closed: when authority, identity, or evidence is missing, the system stops rather than guesses. Platforms prove themselves to one another with their own identity credentials rather than trusting where a request came from, and consequential actions carry signed receipts at headquarters scope. The architecture is native by design — the house's cores are its own, running on the house's own sovereign infrastructure under headquarters' control; outside products appear only as optional, detachable, labeled tools at a platform's edge, never as its foundation.

Privacy protections are global by design: the same discipline applies in every jurisdiction the house serves, at the strictest standard the house keeps — not the loosest a locality allows.

THE DOCTRINE

How the Program Stands

Reference-Only

Platforms hold references and workflow state, not personal records; care platforms hold no health data at all.

Glass-Box

No record, no action — every consequential step is recorded, receipted, and answerable at headquarters.

Fail-Closed

Missing authority or evidence stops the system; the house's platforms never guess their way forward.

Sovereign & Native

The house's own cores on the house's own infrastructure — data sovereignty is architectural, not promised.
