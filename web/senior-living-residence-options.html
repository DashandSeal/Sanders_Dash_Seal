Residence Options & Floor Plans

The residence options of the company — and how each one is presented, walked through, and confirmed in writing.

A residence is chosen with the eyes, not from a diagram. Sanders Senior Living LLC presents its residence options in the admissions journey — in person, on a tour, and in writing — so a family chooses from the real rooms and the real terms, not from a rendering.

Presented in Person, Confirmed in Writing

This page publishes no floor plans, because the record of this company holds none to publish — and this house does not invent what it cannot show. What it does instead is a standing rule: every option a family considers is presented in person, and the option a family chooses is confirmed in the written engagement before anyone moves. The three paths through the house — independent living, assisted living, and memory care — are set out on their own pages; the tour is where they become a home.
