Facility & Site Security

A site held to standard for every hour it operates — access points, rounds, and logs, under written post orders.

A facility is a hundred small disciplines repeated daily: the door that must be checked, the visitor who must be signed, the round that must be made at the hour it is owed. Facility and site security is S & G Security Inc.'s standing-post work for buildings and grounds — the ongoing presence that keeps a site's own rules real.

The engagement defines the site: its access points and who passes them, its rounds and what they inspect, its hours of coverage, and the record each shift owes. Officers work to the site's post orders and the facility's own rules together — a site with a sign-in discipline gets officers who keep it; a site with restricted areas gets officers who know exactly where they are.

Facility engagements run across the settings on this website, including the house's own kind of ground: healthcare facilities and residential communities, each with their own pages here. Every facility engagement is scoped at consultation and confirmed in writing.

How a Facility Engagement Stands

Access Points Held

Doors, gates, and desks worked to the site's own rules — who passes, and on what basis, is never improvised.

Rounds Made and Logged

Rounds inspect what the orders name, at the hours the orders set, and the log proves them.

The Site's Rules, Kept

Officers are briefed on the facility's own disciplines and keep them as their own.

A Record Per Shift

Every shift closes into the client's record: conditions, visitors of note, incidents if any.
