Light Housekeeping & Laundry

The light work that keeps a home livable — tidying, laundry, fresh linens, and the rooms a client actually lives in.

A cared-for person deserves a cared-for home. Light housekeeping in this company means the upkeep of the client's own living areas — the bedroom, bathroom, kitchen, and living spaces a client uses every day — kept clean and orderly as part of a care visit, not as a separate cleaning service.

WHAT IT COVERS

A Home Kept Livable

Tidying & Surfaces

The client's living areas tidied and surfaces kept clean, visit by visit.

Laundry & Linens

Clothing and bed linens washed, dried, and put away — fresh sheets on the bed.

Kitchen & Bath Upkeep

The two hardest-working rooms in the house kept clean as part of care.
