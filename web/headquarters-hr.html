WHAT HR MEANS HERE

Administration for a Contractor House

The Sanders Companies engage independent contractors only — there are no employees, and these portals do not pretend otherwise. What a company would call human resources is, here, contractor relations: the administration of how contractors come to the house, the standards they work under, the records that stand behind them, and the doors they use when something is wrong.

Onboarding runs company by company — caregivers through Home Healthcare's network, officers through S & G's corps — under each company's published standards. The handbook of record stands at headquarters, and a contractor's acknowledgment of it is itself a record, kept with its receipt. Questions of administration route through the support portal as tracked cases, answered by a person.
