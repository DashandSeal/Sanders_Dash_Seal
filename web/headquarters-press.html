Press & Media Kit

Approved brand assets and facts for media — the house's identity, exactly as issued.

The brand assets of the Sanders Companies are the four official seals, displayed in the footer of every page of this website. They are the house's identity, and the rule for their use is absolute: a Sanders seal is used exactly as issued — never redrawn, re-created, approximated, or altered in any medium, by anyone. Media requiring seal files request them from headquarters through the Contact page; no other rendering of a Sanders seal is authorized.

Facts for Media

The Sanders Companies are a family house of four companies under one corporate headquarters: S & G Security Inc. (corporate headquarters, Est. 1996), Sanders Home Healthcare & Caregivers LLC (Est. 2025), Sanders Senior Living LLC (Est. 2025), and Sanders Legal Helpers LLC, doing business as Sanders Dash & Seal (Est. 2025). The founder and owner of the house is Roosevelt Sanders. The house's platform architecture, the Sovereign RTSA Ecosystem, counts 140 distinct platforms designed for interconnected human and commercial workflows, global by design across seven continents and 195 countries and territories.

The house's standard lines, for attribution: “Our family will take great care of your family.” “Four Companies. One Family. One Future. One Commitment.” “Global By Design. The Sanders Way!” Media inquiries are taken through the Contact page of this website.
