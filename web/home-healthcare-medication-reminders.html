Medication Reminders & Wellness Checks

Reminders that keep a daily routine on time, and a caregiver's eyes on how the day is going — recorded at every check-in.

A medication routine only works if it happens on time, every day. Caregivers in this network remind — they do not administer medication. The reminder is given, the routine is kept, and the visit is recorded through the platform's check-in, so the day's care is a matter of record, not memory.

Wellness Checks

Each visit is also a wellness check: how the client is eating, moving, resting, and feeling, observed by the caregiver and recorded. What a caregiver sees is what the family and the care plan get — through the record, not through recollection.
