SANDERS SENIOR LIVING LLC

Safety & Security

A house kept safe by the headquarters company itself — security is not a vendor here; it is the family name.

Sanders Senior Living LLC stands under the corporate headquarters of S & G Security Inc. — a security company. The safety of this house is therefore not a contract bought from strangers; it is the founding discipline of the house's own headquarters, applied at home.

Recorded, Not Assumed

The ecosystem's rule is no record, no action — and safety keeps it. Check-ins, decisions, and outcomes on the company's platform are receipted records, reviewed by human operators; the platform fails closed rather than guessing. A family's questions about safety are answered the same way everything here is answered: by name, in the open, and against the record.
