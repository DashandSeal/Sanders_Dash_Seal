SANDERS HOME HEALTHCARE & CAREGIVERS LLC

Our History

This company was not founded on a market study. It was founded on a friendship — and on a man who deserved to be heard.

A Friend Who Needed to Be Heard

In 2025, the founder’s friend was in the early stages of dementia, and it hurt the founder that nobody would listen to him. All his friend wanted was to be heard, understood, and respected. When they met, the friend asked him if he was real — and the founder told him yes: “I’m a real boy. You can even touch me — see, I’m not Pinocchio.” The friend was going through legal battles the founder calls unfair; the details are the friend’s, and they stay his. What the founder saw was simpler and worse: people looking at a man and seeing only his condition. “If all you see is the condition, you never see me — and that hurts worse than the actual infliction.”

Born in Three Weeks

Sanders Home Healthcare & Caregivers LLC was born between August 27 and September 9, 2025 — within about three weeks, perhaps less, as the founder tells it — together with Sanders Senior Living LLC and Sanders Legal Helpers LLC. The three companies were born because they were what his friend needed: help, belief, and people who would listen. And the founder could see that others would need the same help, for the same reason: there is nothing worse than not being heard when you are going through something.

Built to Be a Care Team, Not a Warehouse

From its first design, this company was built against the warehouse model. The founder’s friend had been sent to wrong places by industries that did not talk to each other — so the founder tied the industries together: one call, documented once, and from the moment the house is alerted, everybody can communicate — the caregiver who gives the medicine, the doctor’s appointment, the transport that will be there. That is what makes an effective care team, and it is the architecture this company runs on to this day.

The Credentials

The founder prepared the way he always prepares: completely. He learned as much as he could about his friend’s condition, and took every course, every class, and every certification he could possibly get — by his account, more than 500 certifications and credentials in a matter of about nine months, earned to operate all three organizations. He is a licensed QMAP, and a notary public in Wisconsin and Colorado. This company’s foundation was laid in those months, and it has not moved: dignity and respect.
