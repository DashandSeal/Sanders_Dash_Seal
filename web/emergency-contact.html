Call 911. If anyone is in danger, hurt, or in need of immediate help, emergency services come first — before this page, before any company, always. Nothing on this portal dispatches anyone, and no submission here is monitored as an alarm.

REACH THE PLATFORM, URGENTLY

The Real Paths

For an urgent matter that is not an emergency in progress, place a support record and say so in the subject. Portal records enter the review queue in the order they are placed and are read by a person; for a matter already scheduled, the appointment record is the fastest path.

PREPAREDNESS, HONESTLY

What SURN Is and Is Not

The house's emergency-preparedness platform is SURN — the Synthetic Universal Response Network — and its role is the preparedness record: assessments, drills, and determinations, kept governed. SURN dispatches no responders, issues no alerts, and contacts no agency. Preparedness is a record kept before the emergency; response belongs to emergency services and to the people on the ground.
