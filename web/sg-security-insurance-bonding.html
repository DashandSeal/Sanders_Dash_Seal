Insurance & Bonding

The facts of record, and the rule that governs the rest: particulars are confirmed in writing, per engagement, before work begins.

This page follows the house rule for facts: it publishes what is of record, and it does not invent the rest. No policy numbers, limits, or bonding figures for S & G Security Inc. are published on this website, because figures of that kind belong in an engagement's own documents — current, specific, and signed for — not on a public page that cannot know which engagement a reader is asking about.

The rule that governs every engagement is stated plainly: the insurance and bonding particulars that apply to an engagement — the coverage the work requires, the certificates evidencing it, and the parties it names — are confirmed in writing as part of the engagement's confirmation, before the first shift is staffed. A client holds the certificates with the confirmation, and any particular a client requires beyond them is a matter for the consultation, where it can be answered with documents rather than adjectives.

That discipline protects both sides of the signature. A company that publishes figures it has not bound itself to in the engagement's own papers is decorating, not assuring — and this company was not built to decorate.
