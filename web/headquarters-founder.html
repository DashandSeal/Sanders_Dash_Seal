Office of the Founder

Roosevelt Sanders — founder and owner of the Sanders Companies. In this house, the founder's office is not ceremonial: it is where the standard is set, and where it is answered for.

Roosevelt Sanders is the founder and owner of S & G Security Inc. and of every company in the Sanders Companies. The house is his: its companies, its platforms, its seals, and its standard. He is also the architect of the Sovereign RTSA Ecosystem — the 140-platform architecture he designed and built, beginning in November 2025 from a single original platform and its six industry codes.

How the Founder's Office Works

Material decisions in the house are the founder's, made by name and on the record. The founder's approval standard is personal: an item waiting on his decision is named plainly, with what his approval does, and nothing consequential in the house runs on blanket approvals or silent assumptions. The deciding standard he has set for the house is what is moral and what is ethical.

Through Sanders Legal Helpers LLC, the founder also practices as a Colorado notary public — the flagship's notary and document work is the founder's own trade, offered to customers through Sanders Dash & Seal. The founder's office is reached through the paths on this website: the contact page, and appointment requests through the platform.
