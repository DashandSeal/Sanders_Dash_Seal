BEFORE THE APPOINTMENT

What a Mobile Signing Needs

Three things make an in-person notarization valid. The confirmation you receive restates each of them for your appointment.

Government-Issued ID

Every signer presents valid, unexpired government-issued photo identification at the appointment. Identity is verified before the act.

Documents Unsigned

Bring the documents complete but unsigned — the signature is made in the notary's presence. Signers and any required witnesses are present together.

The Itemized Quote
