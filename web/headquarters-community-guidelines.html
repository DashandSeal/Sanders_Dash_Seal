THE SANDERS COMPANIES · THE RULES OF THE HOUSE

Community Guidelines

The rules every upload, story, and submission on the house's portals stands under — consent, dignity, and a person's review before anything is public.

THE GUIDELINES

Version of Record — 2026-11-25

Consent is the first rule. Place only what you have the right to share: your own story, or the story and photograph of a person whose consent you hold — for a veteran who cannot consent, the family's. A tribute without its recorded consent is never placed, and consent can be withdrawn by writing to the house, which removes the tribute.

Dignity and respect govern content. The foundation of the corporations is dignity and respect; the portals carry nothing that demeans a person, a family, a veteran, or a community. Stories are told about people, never at their expense.

Clients are never named. The house's standing rule: the people the companies serve are not content. Do not place a client's name, image, or circumstances in any submission.

Nothing publishes itself. Every submission stands AWAITING_HUMAN_REVIEW until a reviewer's recorded decision. Approval places a tribute on the wall; rejection closes the record with its reasons on file. There is no automatic publication, no algorithmic promotion, and no comment thread — the portals are records, not feeds.

Accuracy is the submitter's duty. Tell the story as it happened. The house reviews for standing under these guidelines; it does not fact-check a family's memory, and a tribute remains the family's words, attributed to the honoree they named.

Privacy stands. A submitter's contact details are never published — they exist so the house can answer. Published tributes carry the honoree's name, the relationship the submitter recorded, the story, and the photograph: nothing else.

Removal. The house may remove any published tribute that is shown to stand against these guidelines, recording the removal and its reason on the case. A family may ask for its own tribute's removal at any time, through the support portal, and it will be done.
