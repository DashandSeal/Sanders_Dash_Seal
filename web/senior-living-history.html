SANDERS SENIOR LIVING LLC

Our History

This company was born because a man deserved to be heard in his own life — and its founder refused to answer that need with a warehouse.

A Friend Who Needed to Be Heard

In 2025, the founder’s friend was in the early stages of dementia, and nobody would listen to him. All he wanted was to be heard, understood, and respected. The founder went through it with him — the appointments, the legal battles the founder calls unfair, the experience of being seen as a condition instead of a person. “The condition is just that — the condition. It’s not who you are. But if all you see is the condition, you never see me — and that hurts worse than the actual infliction.” A residence built by a man who had watched that happen would be built differently. This one was.

Born in Three Weeks

Sanders Senior Living LLC was born between August 27 and September 9, 2025 — within about three weeks, perhaps less, as the founder tells it — together with Sanders Home Healthcare & Caregivers LLC and Sanders Legal Helpers LLC. The three companies were what his friend needed in order to be helped and believed in. And the founder could see that others would need the same: a place where being heard is the foundation, not an amenity.

Not Another Warehouse

The founder’s words for this company’s design are exact: not just another warehouse for senior living. A warehouse stores; a house cares. So this company was built as a care team that communicates — from the moment the house is alerted to a need, the caregiver, the medicine, the appointment, and the transport are one connected journey, because the founder had already tied the six industries of care together into one platform with one identifier. The person at the center of it stays a person: respected, dignified, and with a say in their own life.

The Preparation

Before this house opened its doors, its founder did the work: every course, every class, every certification he could possibly get — by his account, more than 500 certifications and credentials in a matter of about nine months, earned to operate all three organizations, alongside his work as a notary public in Wisconsin and Colorado and a licensed QMAP. The standard those months produced is the standard of this website: dignity and respect, lived daily.
