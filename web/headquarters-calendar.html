Corporate Calendar & Events

Enterprise dates of record — and the rule by which the house's dates are published.

The corporate calendar of the Sanders Companies is published the way the house publishes everything: a date appears here when it is set of record. Events are not listed in anticipation, and dates are not promised ahead of the record; when headquarters sets a date — a rollout wave, a corporate observance, an enterprise milestone — it stands here with its date, and announcements of it stand in the Newsroom.

Standing Dates of Record

1996 — S & G Security Inc. established; the founding of the house. 2025 — Sanders Home Healthcare & Caregivers LLC, Sanders Senior Living LLC, and Sanders Legal Helpers LLC established under the headquarters. November 15, 2025 — the founder begins the platform architecture that becomes the Sovereign RTSA Ecosystem. November 25, 2026 — the Permanent Bloodline Protection Directive adopted; the design system declaration and website scope directive locked into the record; the first company websites completed to the enterprise standard.

Company-level events — community events at the residence, caregiver network dates, and the like — stand on the companies' own websites, where they belong.

THE RULE

How Dates Stand

Set of Record

A date is published when headquarters sets it — the calendar carries no speculative dates.

Announced in the Newsroom

Enterprise dates of consequence are announced of record in this website's Newsroom.

Company Events at the Companies

Each company's own calendar stands on its own website, under its own name.
