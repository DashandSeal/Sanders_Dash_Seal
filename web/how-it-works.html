THE JOURNEY

Discovery to Delivery,
One Record.

Every engagement at Sanders Dash & Seal travels the same road: a service chosen, a request placed, a human review, an itemized quote, a secure checkout, the appointment itself, and a record that remains. This page is that road, step by step.

Begin at Step One → The Client Portal

Appointment requests are reviewed and confirmed separately. Quotes itemize notarial acts, travel and other services.

THE FIRST STEPS

Choose, Request, Review

The front half of the journey happens in the portal, at your pace.

1 · Choose the Service

Mobile Notary, Remote Online Notarization, or Document Support — the three elections at booking, each with its published starting price.

2 · Sign In & Request

The request asks for the service, the state, the city or neighborhood, and your preferred date and time. No documents are uploaded during intake.

3 · Human Review

Every request is reviewed by a person — the service, the jurisdiction, and the scope confirmed. Nothing is promised before the review confirms it.

THE MIDDLE

Quote, Checkout, Appointment

Money moves only against an accepted quote; the appointment happens only as confirmed.

4 · The Itemized Quote

Your quote appears in your portal timeline, each charge identified — notarial acts, travel, and other services.

5 · Accept & Check Out

Accepting the quote continues to secure checkout. Payment settles against the accepted quote through the platform's payment system.

6 · The Appointment

Mobile: the notary arrives where the signing is set. Remote: the session opens on the platform's own system, where authorized. Either way, identity is verified before the act.

THE END THAT ISN'T

The Record Remains

Delivery is not the end of the journey. It is the start of the record.

7 · Journaled

The act enters the notary's journal and the platform's evidence record — date, parties, and acts performed.

8 · Receipted

The engagement stands in your portal history: request, quote, payment, and completion under one account.

9 · Requestable

Records of an act are requested through the Notary Records page — in writing, with identity verified before release.
