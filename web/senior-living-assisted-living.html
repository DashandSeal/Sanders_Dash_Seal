SANDERS SENIOR LIVING LLC

Assisted Living

Senior living with assisted care — help at hand for the daily work of living, dignity first in every detail.

Assisted care is half of this company's recorded purpose: Sanders Senior Living LLC was founded for senior living and assisted care. Assisted living joins a residence of one's own to personal assistance with the daily work of living — the help a person needs, given the way this family gives everything: personally, and with respect.

Help, Measured by Honor

Assistance here is never a routine performed on a person. It follows a written care plan, built from an assessment and reviewed as needs change, and it is recorded on the company's platform — every plan a record, every record answerable. The standard does not move: does it honor the person it touches? If it does not, it does not ship.
