FAQ

Engagements, officers, reporting, and records — the questions the company is asked, answered from the record.

What does S & G stand for?

Sanders and Greer. The company was founded in 1996 by Roosevelt Sanders, who asked Willie Greer to start it with him. Her initial remains in the name permanently.

How does an engagement begin?

With a consultation request — through the Sanders Dash & Seal platform's appointment door, or this website's contact page. Then, in order: human review, a consultation, and a written confirmation of scope, orders, and terms. No engagement is staffed from a web page, and none is quoted by a machine.

Is S & G the same as the corporate headquarters site?

One company, two doors. S & G Security Inc. IS the corporate headquarters of the Sanders Companies — and it is also an operating security company. This website is the operating company's own; the headquarters site, at /headquarters, carries the house's corporate presence. Both are S & G; neither is a copy of the other.

Where does the company serve?

Where an engagement is confirmed in writing. The company was founded in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, and the companies under its headquarters are seated in Colorado; an engagement's area is named in its own confirmation, and the company does not take ground it cannot staff to standard.

Are your officers employees?

No. The Sanders Companies staff on one model across all four companies: independent contractors, screened, trained, and supervised to the house standard.

How do I receive reports from an engagement?

Through the client portal, under your sign-in: shift reports, patrol logs, and incident records are delivered into the engagement's record as they are made.

How do I report an incident?

Clients report through the client portal or this website's contact paths; the report enters the same record officers write into. In an emergency in progress, call emergency services first — always.

What are the company's licensing facts?

Of record: a Wisconsin corporation, founded 1996, Wisconsin Department of Financial Institutions entity S049302. The licensing particulars of a specific engagement are confirmed in writing within its confirmation.

What insurance and bonding does the company carry?

Particulars are confirmed in writing per engagement, with certificates delivered at confirmation. This website publishes no policy figures — by discipline, not by omission.

Does the company publish its clients?

No. The company's first client in 1996 was never named, at the founder's standing choice, and the rule has not changed: clients are not content.
