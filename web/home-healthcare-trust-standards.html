Trust & Background Standards

Trust here is a system, not a promise: verified identifiers, recorded assignments, and receipted access — no record, no access.

A family does not hand its home to a promise; it hands it to a system. In this company the system is the platform: caregivers and clients are held as verified identifiers, every assignment is a record made before care begins, and every read of an intake or assignment record passes the platform's access guard — guarded by role and by per-record ownership — and is receipted before the record is served.

That discipline is the background a Sanders caregiver works against: who they are is verified in the system, where they are assigned is a matter of record, and what happens on a visit is checked in, not remembered.

THE SYSTEM

Verified. Recorded. Receipted.

Identifiers

Caregivers and clients are held on the platform as verified, opaque identifiers — references, not dossiers.

Assignments

No caregiver enters a home on a word. The assignment is a platform record, made in advance.

Receipts

Every guarded read of an intake or assignment record is receipted before the record is served.
