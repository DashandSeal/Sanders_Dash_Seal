Accessibility Statement

The enterprise accessibility commitment — part of the Royal Enterprise Standard, not an afterthought to it.

Every page of the Sanders Companies' websites is built to be reached, read, and used by everyone: semantic structure that assistive technology can follow, navigation that works by keyboard as by touch, text alternatives for the seals and imagery that carry the house's identity, and responsive presentation at desktop, tablet, and mobile sizes alike. Accessibility is written into the founder's development standard for every page of the enterprise, alongside privacy and the Royal Enterprise Standard itself.

When a Page Falls Short

A barrier on any page of the house's websites is a defect, and it is reported like one: raise it through the Contact page or the Ethics Reporting Channel, and it is recorded, reviewed at headquarters, and answered for. The house's commitment is not that its pages are perfect; it is that accessibility is a standing requirement of the standard, measured on every page the enterprise builds.
