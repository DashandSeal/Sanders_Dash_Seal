SANDERS SENIOR LIVING LLC

Privacy

How this company and its platform treat information — as recorded in the platform's own design.

This website presents Sanders Senior Living LLC. The website itself collects nothing: its pages are served by the Sanders Dash & Seal platform, and appointment requests are made through that platform's appointment path.

Reference Only, by Design

The company's platform carries care-coordination records by reference only. It holds no resident names, no health data, and no care notes — references and workflow state only, the same discipline as across the Sanders ecosystem.

Determinations on those records are records placed before human operators, recorded against presented evidence. The platform operates no residence, admits no one, discharges no one, and provides no care itself.

This page states the discipline as it is recorded in the platform's design. The company's full legal privacy policy wording is issued by the company and its counsel.
