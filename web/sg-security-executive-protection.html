Executive & Corporate Protection

Close, quiet protection for people and proceedings — planned in advance, staffed discreetly, and confirmed personally.

Some security is meant to be seen. This is the other kind: protection that stays close, stays quiet, and keeps a person's day — or a company's proceeding — moving without incident and without spectacle. S & G Security Inc. provides executive and corporate protection by engagement, planned with the client in advance and confirmed personally.

The work is planning first: the person's schedule, the settings they move through, the proceedings that draw attention, and the points where a quiet presence matters. Officers assigned to protection work are selected for discretion as much as for presence — the standard is that the protected day feels ordinary, and only the plan knows why.

Protection engagements are the company's most deliberately taken work. They are scoped in consultation, staffed by name where the engagement requires it, and confirmed in writing; particulars of any person's protection are never published, here or anywhere — discretion is not a feature of this service, it is the service.

How Protection Work Stands

Advance Planning

Schedules, settings, and proceedings are planned before the day — protection is preparation, not reaction.

Close and Quiet

Staffed Deliberately

Officers chosen for discretion and judgment, and briefed on the person and the proceeding.

Nothing Published

No client, no itinerary, no particular is ever made public. Discretion is the service.
