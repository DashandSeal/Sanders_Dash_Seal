AT INTAKE

What Is Never Collected

The journey is designed to need as little of your life as possible.

No Documents at Intake

The booking request uploads nothing. It asks for the service, the state, the place, and the time — the documents themselves appear at the appointment, in the notary's hands.

The Minimum Necessary

Each step of the journey collects what that step requires and no more — the enterprise's privacy standard, applied to the flagship first.

Consent on the Record

Verification at intake happens under the consent stated in the booking form itself. Nothing is checked silently.

IN THE RECORD

Held by Reference, Sealed by Fingerprint

The platform's records are built to prove without exposing.

Reference & Fingerprint

Engagement records persist references and cryptographic fingerprints of documents and sessions — enough to prove what happened, without warehousing the family's paper.

The Glass-Box Rule

No record, no action: an act that cannot be evidenced is not performed. Every completed engagement leaves its trail — request, quote, payment, journal entry.

Fail-Closed Gates

The platform's sensitive capabilities sit behind named authorization gates that deny by default. A capability that is not affirmatively authorized does not run.

THE HOUSE STANDARD

One Discipline, Four Companies

The flagship's privacy posture is the headquarters standard, stated on the Headquarters website's Privacy & Security Program page.

Native Infrastructure

The platform runs on the house's own architecture — its records are not a feature of someone else's product.

Signed Accountability

Executive actions on the platform carry cryptographic receipts under the house's integrity system, so who authorized what is provable, not remembered.

HIPAA-or-Equivalent, Globally

The enterprise standard is health-grade privacy discipline in every jurisdiction the house serves — by design, not by local exception.
