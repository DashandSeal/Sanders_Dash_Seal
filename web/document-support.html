THE BOUNDARY, KEPT CLEAR

Administrative — Not Legal Advice

Document support prepares paper. It does not advise on what the paper should say.

What It Is

Assistance assembling, preparing, and organizing documents for execution — and the notarization that completes them, when a notarial act is required.

What It Is Not

Sanders Dash & Seal is not a law firm, and document support is not legal advice. Questions of legal effect belong with your attorney; the document you bring is the document that is supported.

Support and notarization are one journey here: preparation first, then the act — mobile or remote — with the whole case on one record.
