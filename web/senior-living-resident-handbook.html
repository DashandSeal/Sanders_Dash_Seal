Resident Handbook & Policies

The rules of the house, in the open — the standing policies a resident and family can hold the company to.

A house with a standard should be willing to state it. This page carries the standing policies of Sanders Senior Living LLC at the level the record holds them; the full handbook itself is a company-issued document, given to every resident and family at move-in, so the whole of it is in the family's hands from the first day.

The Standing Policies

The dignity standard governs every policy beneath it: a resident is honored in how they are addressed, assisted, and answered. Care follows the written care plan and its reviews. Family access and visiting follow the family standard — our family takes care of your family, and families are welcome in the life of the house. Resident information is held under the company's recorded privacy discipline, by reference and receipt, never loosely.
