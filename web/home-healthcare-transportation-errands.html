Transportation & Errands

Rides to the appointments that matter, and the errands that keep a household running — one of the company's six recorded service lines.

Getting there is half of getting care. Transportation is one of the six service lines this company was designed around — Non-Emergency Medical Transportation, carried in its platform under industry code 485991 — and a doctor's appointment is the designed entry point of the whole company's care: the ride is where the journey begins.

The same line covers the errands of a household — the pharmacy, the grocery store — and the company's courier line carries healthcare deliveries under code 492110.
