Safety Resources & News

Working guidance from the security company of the house — and the company's news, posted as it is made of record.

A security company learns things standing posts that other people only read about. This page carries the working guidance S & G Security Inc. gives its own clients and officers — plain, practical, and drawn from the practice of the work rather than from theory. It is guidance, not a service: where a need is real, the answer is an engagement, and the consultation page is the door.

Company news stands here on the same discipline as everything else in the house: announcements are posted as they are made of record — engagements the company may name, standards it adopts, milestones of the house — and nothing is posted ahead of the fact. A quiet page means a quiet record, and the company prefers both to noise.

THE GUIDANCE

Working Notes From the Post

Securing a Site After Hours

Walk the perimeter as a stranger would: the open gate, the dark corner, the door that looks locked. Most losses enter through a condition somebody stopped seeing.

Running a Door

A door is a decision repeated all night: who passes, on what basis, recorded how. Settle the three before the event, and the door runs itself.

Writing a Report That Answers

When, where, who, what — in that order, in facts, before the shift closes. A report written from memory a week later is a story, not a record.

Preparing for a Security Walk

Before an assessment: gather the incidents you have already had, the hours the place stands empty, and the things you would most hate to lose. The walk starts from your answers.
