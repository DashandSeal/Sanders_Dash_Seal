THE REQUEST

Choosing RON at Booking

The request itself is the same five answers as any booking — with the remote election made.

The Election

Remote Online Notarization (RON) is offered in the booking form from $150. Choosing it states your preference; the platform's review does the rest.

The Jurisdiction

Name the state of service. Remote notarization is authorized jurisdiction by jurisdiction — the review confirms whether the remote method stands for yours.

The Review's Answer

Confirmation, the itemized quote, and the session's arrangements arrive through your portal timeline. If the remote method is not authorized for your jurisdiction, the reviewer confirms the mobile alternative — your choice governs the path.
