Privacy

How this company and its platform treat information — as recorded in the platform's own design.

This website presents Sanders Home Healthcare & Caregivers LLC. The website itself collects nothing: its pages are served by the Sanders Dash & Seal platform, and appointment requests are made through that platform's appointment path.

HIPAA by Design

The company's platform is HIPAA-by-design, by construction. It stores references and workflow state only: client references and caregiver references are opaque identifiers, and there are no fields for names, diagnoses, or free-text health information anywhere in its schema.

Minimum-necessary access is enforced by role scopes together with a per-record ownership check. Every read of an intake or assignment record goes through the platform's access guard and is receipted before the record is served — no record, no access.

This page states the discipline as it is recorded in the platform's design. The company's full legal privacy policy wording is issued by the company and its counsel.
