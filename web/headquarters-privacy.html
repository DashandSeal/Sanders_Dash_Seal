Privacy

How headquarters and the house treat information — the recorded discipline of the Sanders platforms.

This website presents S & G Security Inc., corporate headquarters of the Sanders Companies. The website itself collects nothing: its pages are served by the Sanders Dash & Seal platform, and appointment requests are made through that platform's appointment path.

Reference Only, by Design

Across the house, the platforms are built reference-only and HIPAA-by-design where they touch care: Sanders Home Healthcare & Caregivers' platform stores references and workflow state only, with no fields for names, diagnoses, or free-text health information; Sanders Senior Living's platform holds no resident names, no health data, and no care notes. Access is guarded, and reads are receipted — no record, no access.

This page states the discipline as it is recorded in the platforms' designs. The companies' full legal privacy policy wording is issued by the companies and their counsel.
