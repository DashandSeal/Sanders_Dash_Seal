THE DOCUMENTS

What Is Brought to the Table

These are the instruments most often presented for notarization in estate work.

Last Wills & Testaments

Execution ceremonies with the witnesses the document requires, present together, identified, and recorded.

Powers of Attorney

Financial and healthcare powers — signed while the principal's direction can be given in person, before the notary.

Advance Directives

Living wills and healthcare directives, frequently signed at hospitals and care facilities — see the page for healthcare facilities.

THE STANDARD OF CARE

Unhurried, and Exact

Estate signings often involve age, illness, or grief. The platform's discipline does not change; its pace respects the room.

Presence & Willingness

The signer appears in person, presents identification, and signs in the notary's presence. A notarization is never performed on a document signed elsewhere.

Witnesses, Arranged

Where the document calls for witnesses, the confirmation names how many are needed so the appointment is complete when it begins.

Administrative Scope

The platform notarizes and supports documents; it does not draft them and does not advise on their contents. Estate documents are prepared by the family's attorney or chosen source.
