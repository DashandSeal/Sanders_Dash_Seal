Start Care

How care begins: a request, an intake and assessment, a care plan, a matched caregiver — then care, with check-ins from the first visit.

Every engagement with this company begins the same way — through the front door of the platform, in the open. Appointment requests are made through Sanders Dash & Seal and are reviewed and confirmed separately; the intake that follows is recorded on the company's platform by reference, under the privacy discipline stated on this website. Nothing about care here starts with a handshake and a hope.

THE PERSON DECIDES

Care Begins With the Person’s Own Yes

One acceptance rule in this company outranks every form: the person being cared for must themselves be at peace with being accepted. A family member’s assurance — “they don’t know what they’re talking about” — is not an answer here, and a power of attorney does not override the person’s own refusal or discomfort. This company will not sign a contract over a person’s no. If the person is uncomfortable or agitated, they are given time to calm down, and they can come back in a couple of days. The founder’s standing words: “I’m not going anywhere. These doors are not going to close because they didn’t walk through them.”

Everything this company is built on exists to make the person being cared for feel respected, dignified, and still have a say in their own life. Readiness is the person’s to decide — not the company’s, and not the family’s. All we can do is wait. That’s integrity. That’s dignity. That’s respect in action.
