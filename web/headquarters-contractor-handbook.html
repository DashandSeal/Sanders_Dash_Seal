THE MODEL

Independent Contractors, Only

The Sanders Companies engage independent contractors. There are no employees of the house, and no page, form, or conversation makes one: an engagement exists only when it is confirmed in writing, with its scope, its standards, and its terms standing in the confirmation. A contractor works under their own direction within the engagement's scope, carries their own obligations as an independent business, and is recorded — Glass-Box, like everything here — for the work the record shows.

Each company's standards govern its own work: caregivers under Home Healthcare's published training and trust standards; officers under S & G's published training standards — screened first, trained before posting, briefed to the post. This handbook binds the model together; it does not replace a company's standards or an engagement's written confirmation.

The record is the relationship's memory: expressions of interest, handbook acknowledgments, engagement confirmations, reports as they are made, spotlight selections — each with its receipt. A contractor reads their own record in the contractors portal, under their own sign-in, and no one else's.

ACKNOWLEDGE

The Acknowledgment of Record

Acknowledging this handbook is itself a record: sign in, acknowledge, and the acknowledgment stands in your contractor record with its Glass-Box receipt, against this version. When the handbook is revised, the version changes, and acknowledgment is asked again.
