Service Area & Coverage

A Colorado company, based in Colorado Springs — and coverage for each home is confirmed at intake, before any engagement begins.

Sanders Home Healthcare & Caregivers LLC is a Colorado limited liability company, based in Colorado Springs. Where a caregiver can be assigned depends on the home, the need, and the network's reach at the time — so coverage is never promised by a map on a website. It is confirmed during intake, for the actual home, before any engagement is entered into.
