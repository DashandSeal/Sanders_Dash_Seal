Risk Assessment & Security Consulting

Know the exposure before you staff against it — an assessment that stands on its own, in writing.

Good security starts before the first officer is posted: with knowing what a place actually holds, where it is actually open, and what would actually hurt it. Risk assessment and security consulting is S & G Security Inc.'s advisory work — a walked site, a written record of exposure, and recommendations a client can act on with S & G or with anyone.

An assessment walks the ground the way an officer works it: entrances and who passes them, lighting and sightlines, perimeters and their gaps, the hours a place is empty, the valuables it holds and where they sleep, and the incidents it has already had. What the walk finds is written as a record — conditions, not impressions — with each exposure answered by a specific recommendation: a post, a patrol pattern, a lock, a light, a procedure.

The assessment stands on its own. A client who takes recommendations elsewhere has still been served honestly; a client who staffs with S & G starts from a record both sides have read. Assessments are arranged by consultation and confirmed in writing, like every engagement.
