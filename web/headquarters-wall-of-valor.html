PLACE A TRIBUTE

Honor a Veteran

Anyone is welcome to place a tribute: a name, a story, a photograph if you have one. Two things stand between a tribute and the wall, and both are deliberate — your recorded consent, and a reviewer's approval. Nothing appears automatically; nothing appears at all until a person has read it and decided it belongs. You will receive a reference ID when your tribute is placed; it is how your record is found, and how you can ask after it.
