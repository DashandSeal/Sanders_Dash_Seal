Corporate Policies Index

The standing policies of record — one index, at headquarters, for the whole house.

This index gathers the standing policies of the Sanders Companies as they are published across the house's websites. Each policy stands on the page that carries it; this index is the register that points to them. Where a policy's full legal wording is required, it is issued by the company and its counsel — the pages here state each policy at the level the record holds it, and say so where that is the case.

Policies are entered into the house's record as the founder rules them, and this index is updated as they land. A policy that is not in this index is not a policy of the house.
