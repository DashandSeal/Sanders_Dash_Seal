Caregiver Training & Standards

The standard is the training: personal, dependable, and answerable — in how a caregiver works, and in how the work is recorded.

A Sanders caregiver is trained to the house standard before the first assignment and held to it at every visit after. Training is grounded in the company's service lines — personal care, safe mobility, medication reminders, meals, housekeeping, transportation — and in the disciplines that surround the work: dignity, discretion, and the check-in record.

Our family will take great care of your family. It is not a slogan on this company — it is the terms on which everyone who wears the name works.
