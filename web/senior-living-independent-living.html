Independent Living

A home of one's own, in a community built on respect — independence kept, good company at hand.

Independent living is for the senior who runs their own day and wants a community around it: a residence of one's own, shared life within reach, and the standing support of the Sanders family of companies behind the door.

Independence, Kept

The measure of this company is the one it keeps everywhere: does it honor the person it touches? In independent living that means the resident's day stays the resident's — their home, their rhythm, their choices — with community, dining, and activities there to join, never to oblige. And if needs change, the path to assisted care runs inside the same house, under the same name, without starting over with strangers.
