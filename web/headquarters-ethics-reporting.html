Ethics Reporting Channel

How concerns are raised to headquarters — in writing, on the record, and answered for.

Any person — a customer, a family, a contractor, a partner, or a member of the public — may raise a concern about the Sanders Companies to headquarters. The channel is writing: through the Contact page of this website, or, for clients of the platform, signed in through the Client Portal. The house takes concerns in writing because the house answers from the record: a concern that is written is recorded, receipted, and reviewed at headquarters — it cannot be lost, and it cannot be quietly set aside.

How a Concern Is Handled

A concern is checked under the house's claim-integrity standard: against the recorded transaction and the records of the matter it raises. The answers the discipline gives are the answers the record supports — consistent with the record, contradicted by the record, or insufficient evidence — and the concern's raiser is answered with what the record shows. Concerns about accessibility, privacy, or the conduct of any company of the house all travel this same channel, to the same desk.

THE CHANNEL

How Reporting Stands

In Writing

Concerns are raised in writing through the Contact page or the Client Portal — so they are recorded, not rumored.

Receipted & Reviewed

Every concern is receipted on arrival and reviewed at headquarters; none is answered from memory.

Checked Against the Record

The claim-integrity standard governs the answer: what the record shows is what the house says.

Open to Anyone

Customers, families, contractors, partners, and the public — the channel belongs to whoever has a concern.
