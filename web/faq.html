BOOKING & PRICE

The First Questions

How an engagement starts, and what it costs.

How do I book?

Sign in and place an appointment request: the service, the state, the city or neighborhood, and your preferred date and time. A human review confirms it — a request is not yet an appointment.

What does it cost?

Published starting prices: Mobile Notary and RON from $150, Document Support from $250, with packages to $20,000 and beyond. Your itemized quote — issued after review, before payment — identifies every charge.

When do I pay?

After you accept the itemized quote in your portal timeline, at secure checkout. Payment settles against the accepted quote; nothing else is charged.

SERVICES & STATES

What Is Offered, and Where

The elections, the map, and the remote question.

Which states do you serve?

The booking set of record is Colorado, Wisconsin, and Arizona. Service availability and notarization methods depend on the applicable jurisdiction and current authorization.

What is RON — and can I use it?

Remote online notarization: appearing before the notary by audio-video session, on the platform's own native system. It opens jurisdiction by jurisdiction, by authorization. Choose it at booking; the review confirms whether the remote method stands for your jurisdiction — and the mobile path stands if it does not.

Do you give legal advice?

No. Document support is administrative preparation assistance. Sanders Dash & Seal is not a law firm; questions of legal effect belong with your attorney.

THE APPOINTMENT & AFTER

Preparation and Records

What to have ready, and what remains.

What do I need at the appointment?

Valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID for every signer; the documents complete but unsigned; any required witnesses present. Your confirmation restates the list for your appointment.

For a remote session?

A camera, microphone, current browser, and a steady connection — the RON Technology Check page walks the list, and the platform's voice and video pages test your device.

How do I get the record of my notarization?

In writing, through the client portal. Identity is verified before any record is released — see the Notary Records & Journal Requests page.
