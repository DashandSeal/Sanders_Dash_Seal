Vendor & Partner Onboarding

How suppliers and partners engage headquarters — verified first, in writing, and on the record.

Suppliers and partners engage the Sanders Companies through headquarters. The path is the same for a first introduction as for a house account: introduce the company and the offering through the Contact page; headquarters verifies identity and standing against the public registers; terms are set in a written engagement; and the relationship is carried on the record from there. The house does not buy, partner, or integrate on handshakes.

What Headquarters Verifies

A vendor's legal identity, its registry standing, and its capacity to deliver what it offers are verified before any engagement. Partners whose products would touch the platform architecture are held to the house's native-by-design doctrine: outside products serve only as optional, detachable, labeled tools at a platform's edge — never as a platform's core, and never load-bearing. A product that cannot meet that standard cannot be integrated, whatever its merits.
