Move-In & Transition Guide

The move itself, planned like everything else in this house: personally, and at the family's pace.

A move late in life is not a logistics problem; it is a life changing address. The transition guide of Sanders Senior Living LLC treats it that way — planned with the family, paced by the person moving, and supported by the house before, during, and after the day itself.

Settling Is Part of the Care

The first weeks matter as much as the first day. The house keeps the new resident's world steady while it becomes familiar: consistent people, the day's rhythm, family close — the same steadiness the whole company runs on. What a family should bring is what makes a room the resident's own: the familiar things, because a residence becomes a home by what is loved in it.

Before the Day
