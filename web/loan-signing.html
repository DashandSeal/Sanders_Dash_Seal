THE APPOINTMENT

What a Loan Signing Involves

The signing agent's craft is accuracy under a checklist. These are its working parts.

The Package

Loan documents arrive from the lender or title company. The appointment executes them in order, with every notarized document identified before the signing begins.

Identity & Presence

Every borrower and signer presents government-issued identification, verified before the act. Signatures are made in the notary's presence.

The Return

The executed package returns by the channel the engagement specifies, and the appointment stands on the platform's record — date, place, and acts performed.

FOR THE PARTIES
