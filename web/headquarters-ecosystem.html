The Sovereign RTSA Ecosystem

140 distinct RTSA platforms: designed to support interconnected human and commercial workflows, from one identifier, in real time.

The Sovereign RTSA Ecosystem is the platform architecture of the Sanders Companies: 140 distinct platforms within a total architectural capacity of 143, each platform a business in its own right — with its own identity, its own industry codes, and its own customers — and all of them designed to work together. The subscription business model converts those operational capabilities into recurring revenue; the house's marketing infrastructure introduces and distributes them; and these websites expose them through complete, functional customer journeys.

Six Industries, One Identifier

The architecture began in November 2025 with one platform and six original industry codes — 621610 (Home Health Care Services), 624120 (Services for the Elderly and Persons with Disabilities), 485991 (Special Needs Transportation), 532283 (Home Health Equipment Rental), 423450 (Medical, Dental, and Hospital Equipment and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers), and 492110 (Couriers and Express Delivery Services). The design point is the connection: at a single appointment, one identifier ties the industries together in real time — the visit, the transport, the equipment, the courier, the care — so a person's workflow crosses industries without ever leaving the house's record.

Every platform in the ecosystem is native by design: its core is the house's own, and outside products serve only as optional, detachable, gated tools — never as a platform's mind, and never load-bearing. Platforms prove themselves to one another with their own identity credentials, and every consequential action is recorded under the house's Glass-Box discipline.
