Frequently Asked Questions

The questions families ask this company, answered with the facts of record.

How does care start?

With an appointment request through Sanders Dash & Seal. Requests are reviewed and confirmed separately; then come intake and assessment, a written care plan, and a caregiver assignment. The full journey is laid out on the Start Care page.

Where does the company serve?

The company is a Colorado limited liability company based in Colorado Springs. Coverage for a particular home is confirmed at intake, before any engagement — it is never promised from a map.

Who are the caregivers?

Independent contractors in the company's caregiver network — the company's recorded workforce model. Caregivers are verified through the platform, assigned by record, and held to the family standard on every visit.

What does care cost?

Cost follows the care plan: services, schedule, and hours are set by the assessment, and the payment arrangement is set in the engagement, in writing, before care begins. This website publishes no rates, because no two plans are the same.

Is this medical care?

The company delivers care at home across its recorded service lines — personal care, home health support, transportation, equipment, supplies, and courier services. Its caregivers give medication reminders; they do not administer medication.

How does a family follow the care?

Through the record: every visit is checked in on the platform, and clients sign in through the client portal to their own record.
