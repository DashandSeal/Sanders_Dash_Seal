Dining & Culinary Program

Meals as part of the day's honor — shared tables, unhurried, in the rhythm of the house.

In a house built on respect, the table is not a service point; it is the center of the day. The dining program of Sanders Senior Living LLC treats meals the way the company treats everything: as something owed to people who have earned it — prepared with care, shared in good company, and never rushed.

The Table, Kept Well

What a resident needs and prefers at the table is part of their record — gathered at admissions, carried in the care plan, and honored at every meal. Families are part of the table's life too: the community's gatherings are set of record on the Community Events page, and families are welcomed into them.
