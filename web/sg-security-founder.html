The Founder

Roosevelt Sanders — founder and owner. He started this company in Milwaukee in 1996, a father of three who had fallen asleep at a job site and woken up knowing it was time to be a boss.

Roosevelt Sanders is the founder of S & G Security Inc. and of every company that now stands under it. He started this company because he was tired of working for other people — and because working for other people had taught him he could do the same work himself, without the hassle of being told what to do when he already knew what needed to be done. And, in his words, his children needed something to believe in about him.

From Employee to Boss

The decision came on a job site. He was working security jobs and going to college, finishing one of his marketing degrees, and he fell asleep at a post. That was enough, he says, to understand that it was time to move from being an employee to being a boss. He asked Willie Greer — the G in S & G — to start the company with him; she said sure; and within about two to three weeks he had the EIN, the Wisconsin registration, his detective’s license, and his training, and the company opened from his home. Its founding ethic has never changed: take a stand and protect your own community, instead of waiting for somebody else to make it better.

THE FOUNDER’S STATEMENT

“Everybody deserves dignity and respect,” the founder says. “It doesn’t matter what you have. A condition doesn’t care if you’re rich or poor — an affliction doesn’t care. What matters is how you are treated, each and every day.”

Disrespect, in this house, is decided by the person who feels disrespected. If you feel disrespected, no one here will tell you that you were not, and no one will explain your feelings away. That is why this house treats a person — never a condition. The condition is not the person.

That standard was this company’s first: a community business, part of the community it protected. It remains the standard of the whole house he went on to build.
