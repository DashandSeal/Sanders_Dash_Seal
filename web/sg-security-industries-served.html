Industries Served

The settings S & G's officers are engaged to protect — each with its own page, its own discipline, and the same standard.

Security is not one trade; it is a family of them, and the setting sets the discipline. S & G Security Inc. takes engagements across the settings below — each answered on this website with its own page, because the officer who suits a hospital corridor is briefed differently from the officer who walks a construction perimeter at 3 a.m.

The list is the company's working ground, stated as it stands: healthcare facilities — including the house's own care companies' settings; residential communities, the founding work; construction sites in progress; events and gatherings; corporate and facility clients with standing posts; and faith communities, where the company's second-ever client was a church and the relationship between a congregation and its security remains a matter of trust as much as coverage.

A setting not named here is not refused by silence: it is answered at consultation, where the company decides — deliberately, and on the record — whether it can serve the need to its standard.
