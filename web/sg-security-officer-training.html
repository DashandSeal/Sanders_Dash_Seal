Officer Training & Standards

Screened, trained, and held to the house standard — the pattern the founder set when he took his own license and training first.

S & G Security Inc. began with a founder who got his detective's license and his training before he took his first client. The order has never changed: credentials and training come before the post, not after the first mistake. This page states the standards every officer of the company is held to.

Officers are screened before they are engaged — identity, background, and fitness for the work checked and recorded, in the same spirit as the trust and background standards the house keeps for its care companies. Training follows: the disciplines of the post — observation, reporting, de-escalation, the law's boundaries for a private officer, and the house's own privacy and conduct standard — and then the briefing that matters most, the one for the specific post: its orders, its people, its escalation path.

Standards are kept by supervision and by the record. Shifts are reported; reports are read; an officer whose work falls below the standard is retrained or removed from the post. The uniform carries the family's name, and the company treats that as literally as it sounds.

THE STANDARD

How an Officer Is Made and Kept

Screened First

Identity, background, and fitness checked and recorded before engagement — no officer is a stranger to the company that posts them.

Trained Before Posting

Observation, reporting, de-escalation, legal boundaries, and the house's privacy and conduct standard — before the first shift.

Briefed to the Post

Every post briefs its own orders, people, and escalation path; general training never substitutes for knowing the ground.

Kept by the Record

Reports are read, supervision is real, and falling below the standard means retraining or removal. The name on the uniform is the family's.
