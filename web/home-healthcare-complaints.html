A Case, Not a Drawer

A complaint placed here becomes a tracked case: a reference ID at once, a standing in the review queue, a human review, and a recorded outcome — resolved on the record, or answered with the reasons standing beside it. The house would rather carry a complaint it can answer than a grievance it never heard. What you write is read by the company it concerns and reviewed at headquarters.

THE DISCIPLINE

How a Complaint Is Worked

Receipt is recorded with its Glass-Box receipt. Review is by a person, at the company concerned, under headquarters' eye. Resolution — what was done, or why nothing could be — is recorded on the case itself, so the record of the complaint and the record of its answer are the same record. Conduct that concerns ethics rather than service stands in the ethics reporting channel as well.
