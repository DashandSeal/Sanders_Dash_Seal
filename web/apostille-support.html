WHO ISSUES WHAT

Stated Exactly

An apostille is a government act. This page describes support — not the issuance itself.

The Issuing Authority

Apostilles and authentications are issued by the Secretary of State of the state where the notary is commissioned. No private company issues them, and this platform does not claim to.

The Notarization Beneath It

An apostille certifies the notary's commission — so the underlying notarization must be performed correctly, by a commissioned notary, in the right jurisdiction. That act is ours to perform.

The Support Around It

Preparation review, correct sequencing, and coordination of the submission — so a document is not rejected for a defect that care would have caught.
