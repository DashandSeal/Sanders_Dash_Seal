Construction Site Security

Tools, materials, and perimeters kept through the night — on a site that is a different site every week.

A construction site is valuable, open, and changing: materials arrive before they are fixed down, equipment sleeps where it worked, and the perimeter that held last month has moved with the build. Construction site security is S & G Security Inc.'s after-hours and standing coverage for sites in progress — the presence that makes a site's nights as accountable as its days.

The engagement follows the build. Post orders name the perimeter as it stands, the materials and equipment of value, the access the site still needs after hours, and the checks each round must make; as phases change, the orders are revised with the site manager — in writing, so the coverage on the ground is always the coverage on the record.

Morning matters as much as midnight: the night's record — rounds made, conditions found, anything disturbed — is handed to the site's day as a written report, so a problem is discovered by reading, not by loss. Construction engagements are scoped per site and phase, and confirmed in writing.
