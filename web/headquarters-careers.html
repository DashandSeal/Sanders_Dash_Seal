S & G SECURITY INC. · CORPORATE HEADQUARTERS

Careers & Contractor Opportunities

The house's workforce is a network of independent contractors — verified, assigned by record, and held to the family standard.

The Sanders Companies work through independent contractors. That is the house's workforce model of record, across every company: the house does not engage employees; it engages verified independent contractors — caregivers, specialists, and professionals — whose identity is verified, whose assignments are recorded, and whose work is held to the same standard as the founder's own.

The Paths In

Caregivers join through Sanders Home Healthcare & Caregivers' caregiver network, where the onboarding journey, training standards, and trust and background standards stand on the company's own website. Sanders Senior Living engages its team the same way, through its own Join Our Team page. Security, platform, and professional contractor opportunities are taken at headquarters through the Contact page, and every engagement — whatever the company — is set in writing before work begins.

THE MODEL

How the Workforce Stands

Contractors Only

The house's workforce model is independent contractors, in every company, without exception.

Verified

Identity and background are verified to the house's trust standards before any assignment.

Assigned by Record

Work is assigned and recorded in the platform — no informal engagements, no unrecorded access.

The Family Standard

Every contractor of the house carries the same promise: our family will take great care of your family.
