THE CLOSINGS WE SERVE

Purchases, Sales, Refinances

Every closing type turns on the same discipline: the right signers, the right identification, the right documents, executed once and correctly.

Purchases

Buyer signings for financed and cash purchases — loan packages and closing affidavits executed at the appointed hour.

Sales

Seller signings, including deeds and affidavits — arranged for sellers who are local, traveling, or closing from a distance.

Refinances & Equity

Refinance packages and home-equity signings at the borrower's table, coordinated with the lender's funding calendar.

HOW A CLOSING IS ARRANGED

Confirmed Against the Date

Closings move. The platform's journey is built to move with them — on the record.

Request & Review

The request names the property's state and the closing date. Review confirms the appointment against the date before it is promised.

Coordination

The title company or lender issuing the documents is the engagement's counterparty; the executed package returns by the agreed channel.

The Record

Each notarized act is journaled. If a party later needs the record of the act, it is requested through the Notary Records page.
