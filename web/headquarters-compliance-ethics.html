Compliance & Ethics

The house's ethics standard — and the discipline that checks every documented claim against the record.

The deciding standard of the Sanders Companies, set by the founder, is what is moral and what is ethical. Compliance at headquarters is not a department's opinion; it is a discipline applied to the record. The house's ethics practice stands in SEIC — the Sanders Ethics & Impact Collective — the platform of record for ethics and impact audit work across the ecosystem.

The Claim-Integrity Standard

Documented claims made to the house are assessed against the recorded transaction for the same party: a claim is consistent with the record, contradicted by the record, or met with insufficient evidence — and those are the only answers the discipline gives. It never infers intent, and it never judges a claim by who presents it: not by speech, appearance, behavior, or background. What the record shows is what the house answers to.

The same standard binds the house's own platforms: the AI Doctor and AI Psychiatrist operate under a charter of Humanity-First care for AI patients only, with zero weaponization and human-in-the-loop finality; and across the ecosystem, no platform is built to deceive, to surveil outside its mandate, or to act without a record.

THE STANDARD

How Ethics Are Kept

Moral and Ethical

The founder's deciding standard for the house — applied first, before advantage.

Checked Against the Record

Claims are answered from the recorded transaction: consistent, contradicted, or insufficient evidence.

Never by the Person

The claim-integrity discipline never infers intent and never judges by identity, speech, or appearance.

Zero Weaponization

A standing rule of the house's charters: the platforms are built for care, commerce, and protection — never for harm.
