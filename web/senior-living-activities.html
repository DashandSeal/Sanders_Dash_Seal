SANDERS SENIOR LIVING LLC

Activities & Enrichment

Days with something in them — enrichment as a discipline of honor, joined always by choice.

A life's work earns more than an empty calendar. The activities and enrichment program of Sanders Senior Living LLC exists so that a resident's day holds what they choose to put in it — gathering, making, moving, learning, celebrating — in the company of a community.

By Choice, Always

Enrichment in this house is an offer, never an obligation: participation is the resident's choice, every time. The calendar itself is a living record of the community — gatherings and events are set of record on the Community Events page, and families are welcomed into the life of the house through them.
