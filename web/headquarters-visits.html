S & G SECURITY INC. · CORPORATE HEADQUARTERS

Headquarters Visits & Directions

Office logistics for a visit to headquarters — by appointment, and confirmed in writing.

Headquarters receives visitors by appointment. A visit is requested through the Sanders Dash & Seal platform's appointment path — the same machinery that carries the house's scheduling — and is reviewed and confirmed separately. The confirmed appointment is the visit's record: it carries the address, the directions, and the time, in writing, so a visitor travels on instructions that were actually issued — never on an address taken from a listing.

What to Know Before You Come

Headquarters stands in Colorado Springs, Colorado, the home city of the Sanders Companies. A confirmed visitor brings photo identification, arrives at the confirmed time, and is received by the office the appointment names. Meetings at headquarters are recorded in the platform like every other working appointment of the house. For anything short of a visit — questions, introductions, documents — the Contact page carries the house's working paths, and most business is done there.
