Emergency Response

When something happens, the response and the record are one — safety first, on written orders, with nothing left to memory.

An emergency is the moment a security engagement earns its cost. S & G Security Inc.'s response discipline is set before the moment arrives: every engagement's written orders name what its officers do when something happens — the safety steps that come first, the people who are called, and the record that is made — so no officer on any post is improvising the company's answer.

The order of the response never changes. Safety first: people before property, always. Then the authorities the situation requires — fire, medical, or law enforcement — notified as the orders direct, with the officer's role being to protect the scene and the people in it until they arrive, and to assist them when they do. Then the client, along the escalation path the engagement sets. And throughout, the record: times, observations, and actions written as they happened.

That record is the house's Glass-Box discipline applied to its hardest hour: an incident that is not recorded did not get answered, and a claim about an incident is checked against the recorded one. Response coverage exists only where an engagement stands — it is scoped with the service it protects, and confirmed in writing with it.
