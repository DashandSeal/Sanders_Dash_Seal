S & G SECURITY INC. · THE SECURITY COMPANY

Join the Officer Corps

Work with the security company of the house — as an independent contractor, held to a family standard.

The Sanders Companies are staffed on one model, everywhere in the house: independent contractors, engaged for their craft and held to the house standard. S & G Security Inc.'s officers — the Officer Corps — work on that model: professionals engaged per the company's needs, screened and trained to its standards, and posted where their judgment fits.

The path is personal, the way the company's engagements are. Interest is expressed through the paths on this website — the contact page, or an appointment request through the platform — and every candidate is considered by a human being. What the company looks for is stated plainly on its training and standards page: a clean record that can be checked, the composure the work requires, and the willingness to be held to a written standard on every shift.

Opportunities are confirmed personally and in writing, as engagements require them; the company keeps no standing application form and makes no promise this page can be read as an offer. What it can promise is the house's side of the bargain: real standards, real supervision, and work that carries a family name worth wearing.
