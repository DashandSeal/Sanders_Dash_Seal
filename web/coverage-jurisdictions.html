METHOD FOLLOWS LAW

Mobile Is Not Remote, in Law

A state that welcomes a traveling notary may govern remote notarization differently. The platform treats the two methods as separate questions.

Mobile Service

In-person notarization follows the law of the state where the act is performed. The review confirms the appointment under that law.

Remote Service

Remote online notarization opens jurisdiction by jurisdiction, by named authorization only. Until a jurisdiction is authorized, the platform does not offer the remote method there — the mobile path stands instead.

Fail-Closed by Design

The jurisdiction framework is fail-closed: an unapproved jurisdiction is a closed one. That is a protection for the signer, not a limitation of the machinery.

GLOBAL BY DESIGN

The architecture is global as a foundation — jurisdictions are structured into the platform before they open, and each opens by authorization, announced as it stands.
