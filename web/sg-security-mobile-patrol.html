Mobile Patrol

Checked, logged, and reported — patrol coverage that moves, for properties that do not need a posted officer around the clock.

Not every property needs an officer standing on it. Mobile patrol is the company's moving coverage: an officer in a marked pattern of visits — doors and gates checked, perimeters walked, lighting and locks noted — with every visit logged and every condition found reported to the client.

A patrol engagement is planned, not improvised. The properties, the checks each visit must make, and the reporting each visit owes are set in writing at the engagement; visit timing is varied within the plan, so the coverage is real without being predictable. What the officer finds — an open gate, a broken light, a door that should not be open — goes on the record the same night, with the client's escalation path followed as the engagement's orders direct.

Patrol suits properties between posts: sites under construction, facilities after hours, residential communities, and holdings spread across more than one address. It is scoped at consultation and confirmed in writing, like every engagement the company takes.
