Admissions & Eligibility

How joining the community works — step by step, personally, and in writing before anyone moves.

Admissions at Sanders Senior Living LLC is a journey with a family's name on it, not a funnel. It begins with a conversation and a tour, and it ends — only when a family is ready — with a written engagement that states the residence, the care plan, and the costs together.

Eligibility, Person by Person

Eligibility is confirmed in the admissions conversation, person by person: the question is whether this house can honor this person's needs — in independent living, assisted living, or memory care — and it is answered honestly. If the answer is yes, the path below is the whole of it; nothing is hidden in a later step.
