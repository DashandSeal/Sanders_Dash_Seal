THE MAN

The Founder’s Own Trade

Roosevelt Sanders is the founder and owner of Sanders Legal Helpers LLC and of every company in the Sanders Companies. Through this company he practices his own trade: he is a notary public in Wisconsin and Colorado, and the notarizations and document work this platform delivers are his — reviewed by a human, itemized in the open, and sealed under the standard his family name carries.

He did not come to the notary’s commission as a business idea. He came to it in a courtroom, standing beside a friend whose own family said he could not do what he had plainly done — because of his condition. The full account stands on this website’s history page. Its ending is the company you are looking at: “No one heard him — so I became a notary.”

THE FOUNDER’S STATEMENT

Dignity and Respect, in His Words

“Everybody deserves dignity and respect. It doesn’t matter what you have. A condition doesn’t care if you’re rich or poor — an affliction doesn’t care. What matters is how you are treated, each and every day.”

Disrespect, in this house, is decided by the person who feels disrespected. If you feel disrespected, no one here will tell you that you were not, and no one will explain your feelings away. That is why this house treats a person — never a condition. The condition is not the person.

And it is why the person themselves decides. The founder will not accept a client who is not themselves at peace with being accepted; a family member’s assurance is not an answer, and a power of attorney does not override the person’s own refusal or discomfort. “Give it a couple of days and bring them back. I’m not going anywhere. These doors are not going to close because they didn’t walk through them.” Readiness is the person’s to decide — all we can do is wait. “That’s integrity. That’s dignity. That’s respect in action.”
