Amenities

The shared spaces and everyday provisions of community life — presented honestly, confirmed on a tour.

Amenities are the provisions of a shared life: the rooms where the community gathers, the spaces where a resident can be quiet, and the everyday services that keep a household running without friction. In this company they are part of the standing offer of the house — and they are presented the way everything here is presented: in person, and confirmed in writing.

What Is Promised Is What Is Recorded

This house keeps one rule for its own descriptions: what is promised is what is recorded. The current amenities of the community are walked through on a tour and set out in the written engagement at admissions — the same document that confirms the residence and the care plan — so a family holds the whole offer in writing before anyone moves.
