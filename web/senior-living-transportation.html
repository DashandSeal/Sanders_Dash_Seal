Transportation Services

Getting there and back — to appointments, to family, to the life outside these walls — arranged and recorded.

A residence is a home, not a boundary. Transportation services at Sanders Senior Living LLC exist so a resident's life keeps its full geography: medical appointments, family visits, worship, errands, and the community's own outings — arranged in advance, and recorded like everything in this house.

Carried by the House's Own Line

The Sanders family of companies carries transportation in its own record: Sanders Home Healthcare & Caregivers LLC holds the house's Non-Emergency Medical Transportation service line, and transportation for residents is arranged through that same family discipline — scheduled ahead, confirmed, and checked in on the platform. A resident's transport needs are noted in the care plan, so getting there is part of the care, not an errand left to chance.
