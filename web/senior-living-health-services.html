SANDERS SENIOR LIVING LLC

On-Site Health & Wellness Services

Health and wellness support for residents — coordinated through the resident's own care plan, and recorded.

A resident's health needs do not pause at the community's door. The health and wellness services of Sanders Senior Living LLC are the support arranged around the resident where they live: wellness check-ins, coordination with the resident's own providers, and care-plan reviews as needs change — each one a record, kept on the company's platform.

Coordinated, Never Assumed

The discipline is the platform's own: records, never assumptions. The company holds care-coordination records by reference — it keeps no resident health data in the platform — and clinical care itself belongs to the resident's own qualified providers, coordinated, never replaced. Where care is needed in a family's own home instead, the house's home healthcare company, Sanders Home Healthcare & Caregivers LLC, is the family's path.

Support Around the Resident

As needs change, the written care plan is reviewed and adjusted — with the family informed through the record.
