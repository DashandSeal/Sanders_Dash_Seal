About the Company

Sanders Senior Living LLC — the senior living company of the Sanders Companies, created to honor the generation that built ours.

Sanders Senior Living LLC is the senior living company of the Sanders family of companies — created to honor the generation that built ours, and held to the same standard our family name carries everywhere it stands: dignity first, in every detail.

Honor for a Life's Work

Doing business with us should feel like the best choice you made that day. For the families who trust us with their own, nothing less is acceptable.

The company is one of the four Sanders companies, under the corporate headquarters of S & G Security Inc. — standing with Sanders Home Healthcare & Caregivers LLC and Sanders Legal Helpers LLC in one house, under one standard: our family will take great care of your family.
