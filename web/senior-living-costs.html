SANDERS SENIOR LIVING LLC

Costs & What's Included

What a family pays, and what it covers — set in writing before anyone moves. No surprises is a standard here.

This page publishes no prices, because this company has none of record to publish — and this house does not invent figures. What it publishes instead is the rule a family can hold it to: the cost of living here is set by the path and the care plan a family chooses, and it is stated in full, in writing, in the engagement — before anyone moves.

Set by the Plan, Stated in Writing

Cost follows the plan: the residence option, the level of assistance in the written care plan, and the services a family elects. What is included is stated with it, line by line, in the same document — so the family holds the whole arrangement in writing, and any change to care, and to cost, comes the same way: assessed, discussed, and recorded. A family's exact figures are given in the admissions journey, where they belong.

THE APPROACH

How Cost Is Set Here

Set by the Plan

The residence, the care plan, and the elected services set the cost — nothing else, and nothing hidden inside them.

What's Included, In Writing

The engagement states what the arrangement covers — the family signs what it has read, and reads what it signs.

Changes in Writing Too

If care changes, cost is re-stated in writing with it — assessed first, discussed with the family, then recorded.
