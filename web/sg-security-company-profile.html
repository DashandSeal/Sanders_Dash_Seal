S & G SECURITY INC. · THE SECURITY COMPANY

Company Profile

S & G's own history and standing — a Milwaukee security company, founded in 1996, that became the headquarters of a family of companies.

S & G Security Inc. was founded in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, in 1996. The name is the founders' own: Sanders, for Roosevelt Sanders, and Greer, for Willie Greer — the woman who agreed, when he asked her, to start the company with him: the two of them did security work for other people, and they could do the same work for themselves. Willie Greer has since passed; her initial stays in the company's name, permanently.

The founder was a father of three, working security jobs while finishing one of his marketing degrees in college. The decision, in his own account, came the night he fell asleep at a job site: that was enough to know it was time to move from being an employee to being a boss. Within about three weeks he had the company's EIN, its registration with the State of Wisconsin, his detective's license, and his training — and S & G opened for business from his home in Milwaukee.

A Community Business From the Start

The work grew the way the founder tells it: one client, then a church, then a business, then people, then communities. S & G was a community business — in an area where the need for security was real and the police, doing a hard job well, were short-handed, the company's founding ethic was to take a stand and protect its own community rather than wait for somebody else to make it better.

That ethic is still the company's standing. Today S & G Security Inc. is the corporate headquarters of the Sanders Companies — Sanders Home Healthcare & Caregivers LLC, Sanders Senior Living LLC, and Sanders Legal Helpers LLC standing under it — and, by the founder's declaration, it retains the Headquarters seal as its own official seal. Its registry fact of record: a Wisconsin corporation, Wisconsin Department of Financial Institutions entity S049302.
