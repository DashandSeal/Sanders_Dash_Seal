Request a Consultation

The intake journey for every engagement: request, human review, consultation, and written confirmation — in that order.

Every S & G Security Inc. engagement begins the same way, and it begins here: with a consultation request. The request is made through the Sanders Dash & Seal platform — the house's scheduling door — or through this website's contact page, and it is answered by a human being. Nothing is quoted by a machine, and nothing is staffed from a form.

A good request carries the shape of the need: the setting (a facility, a community, a site, an event), the ground to be covered, the hours or dates the need runs, and what has already happened that makes security a question now. From there the sequence is fixed: human review, a consultation — a real conversation about the ground, the risks, and the service that fits — and then a written confirmation that sets the engagement's scope, its post orders or patrol plan, and its terms, before any officer is posted.

Where the honest answer is that S & G is not the right instrument for a need, the consultation says so; the company's risk assessment stands as its own service for exactly that reason. Deliberate is not a delay tactic here. It is how the company has taken its work since 1996.
