Frequently Asked Questions

The questions families ask this company, answered with the facts of record.

How does a family begin?

With a conversation and a tour. Tours are requested through the Sanders Dash & Seal platform and confirmed separately; admissions then matches the person's needs to the right path — independent living, assisted living, or memory care — and confirms residence, care plan, and costs in a written engagement.

What living options does the company offer?

Three paths under one roof and one standard: independent living for the self-directed senior, assisted living with personal assistance and a written care plan, and memory care with consistent caregivers and steady routines. Each has its own page on this website.

What does it cost?

Cost follows the plan — the residence option, the care in the written plan, and the services a family elects — and it is stated in full, in writing, before anyone moves. This website publishes no figures, because the company has none of record to publish; a family's exact figures are given in the admissions journey.

Can care change as needs change?

Yes — that is what the written care plan is for. Plans are reviewed as needs change, and a move from independent to assisted living happens inside the same house, under the same name, without starting over.

How does the family stay involved?

Through the family standard and the family portal: families are welcomed into the life of the house, and the care is a record the family can sign in and see — plans, reviews, and outcomes.

Is the company registered?

Yes. Sanders Senior Living LLC is a Colorado limited liability company — Colorado Secretary of State ID 20258000764, established 2025 — with National Provider Identifier 1023988623, standing with its sister companies under the corporate headquarters of S & G Security Inc.
