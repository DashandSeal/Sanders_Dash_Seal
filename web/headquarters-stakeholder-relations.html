S & G SECURITY INC. · CORPORATE HEADQUARTERS

Stakeholder Relations

Communications for stakeholders and agencies — grantor relations included — held at headquarters.

Headquarters is the point of contact between the Sanders Companies and their stakeholders: grantors, agencies, partners, and the communities the companies serve. A stakeholder of any company of the house reaches the whole house through the Contact page of this website, and communications of consequence are recorded and answered from headquarters.

Grantor Relations

The house's federal standing is a matter of public record: Sanders Home Healthcare & Caregivers LLC maintains an active SAM.gov registration (UEI QW8BRKNCF573, CAGE 27KB6) and has submitted research applications under federal programs addressing the abuse, neglect, and exploitation of older adults — the company's own subject, carried into research. Grantor and agency communications are conducted by headquarters on the company's behalf, against the record, with the company's registry facts published in the Corporate Registry on this website for any stakeholder to verify independently.
