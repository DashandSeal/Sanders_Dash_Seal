Sanders Sentinel — Real-Time Protective Intelligence
His architecture must support:
1. Live visual perception: Analyze authorized security camera feeds and voluntarily shared live video to recognize visible hazards as they develop.
2. Real-time environmental awareness: Detect smoke, fire, flooding, hazardous conditions and other immediate physical dangers through connected sensors.
3. Live acoustic detection: Recognize emergency alarms, cries for assistance and distress sounds through authorized audio systems.
4. Real-time medical awareness: Receive supported medical-device and wearable alerts as they occur.
5. Global threat awareness: Continuously process official emergency warnings, severe weather alerts, natural disasters and public safety advisories.
6. Predictive hazard recognition: Identify developing patterns of danger from available evidence rather than waiting until an emergency has fully developed.
7. Multisensor intelligence: Combine authorized visual, acoustic, environmental and emergency information to assess the situation.
8. Immediate protective activation: Initiate appropriate emergency assistance upon detecting credible danger, without depending exclusively on a person requesting help.
9. Continuous situational assessment: Evaluate changing conditions throughout an emergency and adapt protective assistance accordingly.
10. Operational redundancy: Maintain alternative detection and communication channels when primary systems fail.
The fundamental architectural requirement is that Sanders Sentinel must possess real-time perception, detection, assessment and protective response capabilities—not simply the ability to receive requests.
