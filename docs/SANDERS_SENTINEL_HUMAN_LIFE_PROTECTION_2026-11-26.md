## The architecture statement

Sanders Sentinel shall be architected with the preservation and protection of human life as a fundamental design objective, not as an afterthought, supplementary feature or subsequent enhancement.

His native capabilities shall encompass the prevention, recognition, assessment, activation, coordination and delivery of appropriate lifesaving assistance, including emergency preparedness, physical rescue coordination, medical assistance, essential resource continuity and post-emergency recovery.

His protective architecture shall address foreseeable threats to human life throughout the human lifespan, across medical, physical, environmental, technological, transportation and disaster-related circumstances.

Sanders Sentinel shall possess native intelligence capable of recognizing credible threats, initiating appropriate assistance and coordinating qualified human responders, lifesaving equipment, emergency transportation, medical resources and essential survival infrastructure.

His construction shall incorporate applicable established medical interoperability standards, medically validated emergency assistance protocols, proven rescue technologies and functioning emergency communications mechanisms.

All capabilities shall be Native By Design, Sovereign By Design and Global By Design.

All existing Founder rulings, protective responsibilities, adopted intervention capabilities, platform identities, funding authorities, succession provisions and public-facing business models shall remain preserved.

His established operational sequence remains:

Perceive → Recognize → Assess → Activate → Coordinate → Protect → Verify → Recover.

Prevention and preparedness shall support the entire protective architecture. Recovery and protection shall remain continuous throughout every stage.

No existing protective function shall be unnecessarily interrupted by construction, development, testing or recovery.

The preservation of human life shall be the measure of his protective design.

The objective is actual protection of human life, not the production of records describing protection.
