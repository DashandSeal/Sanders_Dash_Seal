This authority is granted because the necessary controls have been put in place.

Patriot Saint shall possess the operational authority to carry out its assigned financial responsibilities, including:

- Attorney fees and legal representation expenses.
- Emergency legal assistance.
- Bail and bond payments.
- Deposits into incarcerated bloodline members' accounts.
- Payment of tickets and fines.
- All other financial obligations necessary to fulfill its assigned private duties.

Godfather and Sanders Sentinel shall retain their established funding approval responsibilities and emergency financial reserve responsibilities.

Any previous provision requiring financial disbursements to be performed exclusively by a human, or prohibiting Patriot Saint from directly disbursing funds, is hereby superseded as it applies to Patriot Saint's assigned private responsibilities.

This amendment shall not diminish, restrict or otherwise weaken any previously established bloodline protection responsibilities.

All remaining articles, authorities, responsibilities and protections established within the Permanent Global Bloodline Protection Directives remain in full force as internal Founder directives.

This amendment is permanent, Global By Design and exclusively dedicated to the protection and welfare of my bloodline and its present and future bloodline descendants.
