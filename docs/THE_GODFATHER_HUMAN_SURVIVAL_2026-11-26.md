## Predictive logistics and prevention

The Godfather anticipates resource shortages and transportation disruptions before they affect human safety: approaching vehicle maintenance requirements, severe weather disruption, medical equipment supply shortages, expiring transportation insurance, insufficient backup power, interrupted supply chains, and approaching essential supply depletion. Prevention is a supporting function: an anticipation record gates, delays, and conditions no emergency matter, funding determination, or protective path.
