# Founder Ruling — Every Platform Must Be Able to Speak

Established 2026-11-26 03:06 MST by Roosevelt Sanders, Founder; specification 2026-11-26 03:09 MST. Preserved verbatim in the founder record (founder-ruling-every-platform-must-speak-2026-11-26.md) and carried verbatim in Sanders Sentinel’s module. This guarded record carries the ruling and its specification for Sentinel’s implementation (band four of the 2026-11-26 Sentinel construction; the fleet-wide rollout to all 140 platforms is a separate build-program item, not this build).

## The ruling, verbatim as given:

"Every single platform must have the ability to speak"

## Founder specification of the ruling — 2026-11-26 03:09 MST, verbatim as given:

"Real-time conversation — natural, interactive, two-way verbal communication.
Multilingual communication — supporting the ecosystem's Global By Design mission.
Native voice calling — incoming and outgoing live voice communications through supported communications infrastructure.
Emergency voice activation — where applicable to the platform's original assigned responsibilities.
Accessibility — preserving text, visual, silent distress, and other communication channels.
Voice continuity and recovery — automatically recovering interrupted voice capabilities while maintaining available alternative communications.
Native infrastructure — no dependency on an external proprietary voice-service provider for the platform's voice intelligence.
Identity preservation — no platform shall lose its individual identity, original functions, industry scope, or established authority through this implementation.
Voice shall be an additional operational capability, never a replacement for existing capabilities or a prerequisite for protective action.
implemented.
All 140 platforms. Distinct identities. Native voices. Two-way communication. Global By Design"

## Founder implementation principle — 2026-11-26 03:11 MST, verbatim as given:

": all 140 RTSA platforms shall possess native, real-time, two-way voice communication under their own distinct identities.
One implementation principle is important: a shared sovereign voice engine may serve all 140 platforms without merging their identities, personalities, responsibilities, or operational authority. Each platform retains its own voice and conversational identity while benefiting from shared infrastructure.
This avoids unnecessarily duplicating 140 separate voice engines while preserving native ownership and architectural independence from external proprietary voice providers.
Live calling remains a required capability, not a verified operational capability. Its development and testing shall not interfere with any existing platform function or protective responsibility."
