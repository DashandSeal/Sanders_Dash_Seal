# Sanders Dash & Seal — Platform Architecture Baseline

Status: Foundation specification. This is not a claim of implemented functionality or regulatory approval.

## Platform identity
- Name: **Sanders Dash & Seal**.
- Business: Sanders Legal Helpers LLC, doing business as Sanders Dash & Seal.
- Intended scope: multi-jurisdictional notarial and related authorized services, with human authority separate from AI identities and service-provider integrations.

## Platinum Blue Diamond principles
- Global intellectual-property protection by design from day one.
- Humanitarian Core Laws and human-in-the-loop control govern all agents.
- Glass-box decisions with inspectable provenance, reason codes, policy versions, and approval records.
- Default-deny authorization, least privilege, identity preservation, immutable evidence, and controlled recovery.
- No automatic assertion that a patent is filed, a signature executed, a notarization completed, or legal authority issued.

## System boundaries
1. Human identity, business identity, and authorized notary credentials.
2. Jurisdiction-specific authorization and service eligibility.
3. Electronic notarization integration with SignWell where approved and lawful.
4. Remote online notarization integration with BlueNotary where approved and lawful.
5. Synthetic-AI orchestration with bounded token leases, human approvals, and audit trails.
6. Evidence preservation, cryptographic integrity, and incident response.
7. Global invention inventory, confidential disclosure controls, assignments, and filing calendar.
8. Deployment, monitoring, rollback, and security testing.

## Authorization safeguards
Colorado, Wisconsin, Florida Commissioner of Deeds, and future jurisdictions must each have distinct eligibility gates. Pending authority must never be shown as issued. Provider onboarding is not itself a notarial commission or approval.
