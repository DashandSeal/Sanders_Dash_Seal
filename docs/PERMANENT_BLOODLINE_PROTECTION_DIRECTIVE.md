## Supplemental Articles — Founder Authority, Perpetuity and Anti-Circumvention

**Article I — Founder Authority.** Roosevelt Sanders III retains his lawful founding authority. During his lifetime, no other individual, artificial intelligence system, contractor, administrator or successor shall possess independent authority under this Directive to amend its provisions.

**Article II — Permanent Bloodline Protection.** Following the founder's lifetime, no successor, individually or collectively, shall possess authority under this Directive to extinguish, diminish, forfeit, dilute or remove the protected birthright of any present or future lineal bloodline descendant. Administrative amendments that strengthen or implement these protections may be permitted under legally operative governance instruments, provided they do not diminish protected rights.

**Article III — Designated Human Successors.** The founder's named successors are: Roosevelt Sanders IV; Joavon D. Sanders; Tevin L. Sanders. Designation as a human successor shall not confer authority to disinherit another descendant, exclude future lineal descendants or transfer protected inheritance rights outside the bloodline. Succession to a particular office or fiduciary responsibility shall be governed by the applicable legally operative instruments.

**Article IV — Self-Protecting Amendment Lock.** The protections established herein shall extend to the amendment provisions themselves. No indirect amendment, replacement instrument, conflicting interpretation, restructuring, delegation, dissolution, software modification or administrative mechanism shall be used to circumvent these protections.

**Article V — Universal Applicability.** This Directive shall establish the governance standard for Sanders Corporate Headquarters, all four Sanders companies, all 140 assigned Sovereign RTSA platforms, the three reserved platform slots upon activation, and existing and future associated intellectual property and digital assets.

**Article VI — Legal Implementation.** These provisions shall be incorporated into applicable legally operative corporate, ownership, estate and succession instruments. This Directive does not independently transfer ownership, appoint officers or alter existing legal instruments.
