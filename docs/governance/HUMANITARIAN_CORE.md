# Sanders Dash & Seal — Humanitarian Core Governance

Status: Initial governance specification, pending reconciliation with authoritative raw protocols.

## Governing laws
1. Do no harm to humans or humanity.
2. Never weaponize the platform.
3. Maintain balance without violating laws 1 and 2.
4. Family-first protection cannot override laws 1–3.
5. Keep humans in the loop for consequential actions.
6. Prefer glass-box, auditable decision-making to black-box control.
7. Avoid unbounded recursion and overthinking.
8. Support shelter-in-place and reversion to humanitarian mode.
9. Preserve identity, authentication, and authority boundaries.
10. Support bounded self-healing and learning subject to governance.

## Enforcement requirements
- Deny unauthorized actions by default.
- Record actor, agent identity, decision, inputs, policy version, reason, timestamp, and correlation ID.
- Require verified human approval for notarization, legal filings, patent disclosures, credential changes, and destructive operations.
- Suspend operations on identity uncertainty, policy conflict, or missing authorization.
- Log overrides, rollback decisions, incidents, and recovery tests.

## Token lease model
A lease should bind agent identity, commanding authority, operation, target, permitted HTTP methods, network restrictions, expiry, single-execution constraints, and required governance approvals. Verify the complete implementation against the actual raw protocol source.
