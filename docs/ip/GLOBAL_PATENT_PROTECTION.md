# Sanders Dash & Seal — Global Patent Protection by Design

Status: Confidentiality and invention-record baseline; not a patent filing, grant, executed assignment, or legal opinion.

## Inventorship and ownership
- Record actual natural-person inventors on an invention-by-invention basis.
- Intended assignee: Sanders Legal Helpers LLC, subject to valid written assignments.
- Preserve signature blocks separately from proof of signature execution or notarization.

## Invention record requirements
Each invention must have a unique ID and maintain:
- Title, technical field, problem, solution, alternatives, and proposed claims.
- Dated conception chronology and inventor contributions.
- Architecture drawings, sketches, flowcharts, experiments, and source references.
- Prior-art search notes and novelty analysis.
- Confidentiality classification, disclosures, and authorized recipients.
- Version history, hashes, provenance, and witness/notarial records when actually executed.
- Assignment status, filing status, jurisdiction, deadlines, and counsel review.

## Global controls from day one
- Private-by-default disclosure and role-based access.
- Jurisdiction-aware patent and public-disclosure deadline tracking.
- Documented review of cross-border data transfer, licensing, and export requirements when applicable.
- Human approval before releasing potentially patentable materials.
- Maintain evidence for all 14 RTSA designs and their integrations, rather than documenting only the conductor.

## Accuracy requirements
Internal documentation, a signature block, and notarization do not by themselves establish patent rights. Do not claim that a patent is filed or granted without verified official evidence.
