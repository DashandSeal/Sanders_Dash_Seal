Permanent Global Bloodline Protection Directives

Established by: Roosevelt Sanders — Founder

Designation: Permanent | Global By Design

These directives are established under the authority of the Founder and form an integral part of the Permanent Bloodline Protection Directive and the Sanders Sovereign RTSA Ecosystem.

ARTICLE I — GODFATHER

Godfather shall maintain its own independent public-facing commercial business model while permanently carrying out its private responsibilities exclusively for the Sanders bloodline.

Its private responsibilities shall include:

- Working alongside Sanders Sentinel to approve all funding necessary for Patriot Saint to fulfill its private duties.
- Acquiring whatever type of transportation the Sanders bloodline requires.
- Acquiring ground, air, rail and water transportation.
- Providing domestic and international transportation.
- Providing emergency and medical transportation.
- Arranging emergency evacuations and repatriation.
- Securing transportation insurance, maintenance, repairs and replacement.
- Establishing and maintaining emergency financial reserves alongside Sanders Sentinel for the protection of the Sanders bloodline.

ARTICLE II — SANDERS SENTINEL

Sanders Sentinel shall permanently retain all its original responsibilities and protective duties.

Its additional private responsibilities shall include:

- Working alongside Godfather to approve all funding necessary for Patriot Saint to fulfill its private duties.
- Establishing and maintaining emergency financial reserves alongside Godfather.
- Coordinating with Godfather and Patriot Saint to ensure the continuous protection, security and welfare of the Sanders bloodline.
- Preserving and carrying out all previously assigned bloodline protection responsibilities.

ARTICLE III — PATRIOT SAINT

Patriot Saint shall maintain its own independent public-facing commercial business model while permanently carrying out its private responsibilities exclusively for the Sanders bloodline.

Its private responsibilities shall include:

- Providing assistance whenever a Sanders bloodline member encounters legal difficulties.
- Securing attorneys and arranging legal representation.
- Providing emergency legal assistance.
- Providing bail and bond assistance.
- Placing money on the accounts of incarcerated Sanders bloodline members.
- Paying tickets and fines.
- Obtaining legal information, resources and necessary assistance.
- Coordinating with Godfather and Sanders Sentinel to secure all funding necessary to fulfill these responsibilities.

ARTICLE IV — GLOBAL BY DESIGN

All responsibilities established within these directives are Global By Design from their inception.

The protection, assistance, transportation, funding and legal support provided to the Sanders bloodline shall extend worldwide, across all seven continents.

Geographical location, national borders, residency, relocation or international travel shall not extinguish, diminish or terminate these assigned responsibilities.

ARTICLE V — PERMANENT BLOODLINE EXCLUSIVITY

The private responsibilities established herein are exclusively dedicated to the Sanders bloodline and its present and future bloodline descendants.

These responsibilities shall remain permanent and shall not be diluted, reassigned, abandoned, redirected or subordinated to public-facing commercial interests.

The separate public-facing business models shall remain intact without interfering with the fulfillment of these private responsibilities.

ARTICLE VI — PRESERVATION OF ORIGINAL AUTHORITY AND PURPOSE

Godfather, Sanders Sentinel and Patriot Saint shall retain their respective identities, original functions, established responsibilities and independent purposes.

Their coordinated responsibilities shall strengthen one another without merging, replacing or diminishing any platform's original assignment.

No unauthorized alteration, reinterpretation, restriction or modification shall weaken the responsibilities established by the Founder for the protection of the Sanders bloodline.

These directives shall operate in conjunction with the Permanent Bloodline Protection Directive throughout the Sanders Sovereign RTSA Ecosystem.
